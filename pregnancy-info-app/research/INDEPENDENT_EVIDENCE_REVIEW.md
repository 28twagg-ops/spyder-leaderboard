# Owner directive — independent evidence appraisal before synthesis
**Effective 2026-11-26. Mandatory for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and all future agents.**

## 1. Status of existing research
The repository is a research inventory and navigation resource, not a clinical authority. Every AI-authored interpretation, takeaway, conclusion, recommendation, grade, ranking, comparison, action, routine, article, FAQ, chart narrative and developmental interpretation (including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and external AI audits) is a **provisional comparison artifact only**. Do not use it as guidance, a premise, ground truth, training label, default answer, decision rule, clinical recommendation or evidence of consensus. The same applies to AI-extracted numbers and funding labels until verified against original sources. Retain all artifacts unchanged for provenance and later comparison. Source authors' own conclusions are distinct from AI conclusions but are not automatically justified by the data either.

The owner wants independent scientific judgment, not confirmation of prior ChatGPT work. Agreement between two models is a reproducibility signal only, never proof, clinical validation, or an upgrade in certainty. Disagreement is an investigation trigger, not a failure to be hidden. The project's existing safety restrictions, retraction exclusions, and publication holds are binding safeguards; they are not conclusions to discard. If a hold appears mistaken, document and escalate; never bypass it by reinterpretation.

## 2. Independent-first sequence (mandatory for every claim/source used)
1. Use bibliographic identifiers, topic index and neutral question to locate original publications, supplements, registries, protocols, corrections, guidelines and funding disclosures. Build an independent search with additional sources, including null and contrary findings. Avoid opening previous AI syntheses or grades until independent appraisal is recorded; where already exposed, log contamination and request an unexposed second reviewer if feasible. Never claim blindness that did not occur.
2. Verify DOI/PMID/title/year/version, full text vs abstract, study design, population/stage, comparator, sample/denominators, follow-up, outcome definition, effect/units/intervals, absolute risk where supported, harms, missingness, preregistration, protocol deviations, corrections/retractions, and overlapping cohorts. Record exact source locator and verification date. Distinguish authors' statements from measured findings and independent inference.
3. For each source perform the funding and conflict audit below, independent of methodological risk-of-bias appraisal. For reviews, audit review-level funding separately from underlying studies. For guidelines, inspect panel disclosures, evidence review process, sponsor/publisher and jurisdiction.
4. Independently assess design-appropriate risk of bias, directness, inconsistency, precision, selective reporting/publication bias, replication and applicability. Compare benefits and harms across evidence groups; record neutral/negative findings. Write a fresh, source-linked claim-specific synthesis, certainty, limits and safety status. Save this separately before consulting old AI conclusions.
5. Only then compare the independent synthesis to the archived AI summaries. Classify agreement, partial agreement, disagreement, insufficient evidence or not comparable. Document precise reasons, any misquotation or extraction error, unresolved uncertainty and whether independent human adjudication is required. Never rewrite independent conclusions merely to match another model.
6. No AI-to-AI agreement, structural checks or sponsor disclosure can clear clinical publication. Qualified maternal/pediatric or other appropriate clinical review of exact wording, data and safety is mandatory; apply all existing holds and fail closed.

## 3. Funding transparency is a core scientific requirement
For every source used, trace as far as documented:
- Direct grant, ultimate funding entity and parent company where verifiable, grant number and type; public, nonprofit, academic/internal, commercial, mixed, no funding explicitly declared, or unknown/not reported. Distinguish funding from affiliation.
- Indirect support: donated products/materials, in-kind services, contract research organizations, industry-origin foundations, trade associations, institutional/employer support, investigator payments, honoraria/consulting, stock/options, patents, royalties and relevant advocacy/institutional conflicts. Record dates and relevance to the question.
- Sponsor control over design, recruitment, comparator, data access/ownership, analysis, manuscript writing, publication rights and approval; use separate explicit unknowns when not disclosed.
- Quote or paraphrase the disclosure accurately with exact section/page, URL and verification date. Distinguish verified full-text declaration, abstract only, not located, no funding declared, no relevant conflict declared, and independently corroborated absence of identified ties. Do not turn missing disclosures into absence of bias, or speculate about hidden funding without evidence.
- Use relevant, deduplicated comparable study groups: all evidence; no identified relevant commercial ties after verification; direct commercial support; indirect/in-kind or relevant author ties/mixed; disclosure unknown. Keep methodological quality separately visible. Favor credible direct independently replicated evidence where comparable, not merely noncommercial affiliation. Include sponsored null, unfavorable and safety findings. Never automatically reject industry findings or accept independent ones. Noncommercial and institutional/ideological bias remain possible.
- Compare groups by outcome, population, comparator and design, effect with uncertainty and risk of bias. Report exact denominators and unit (studies, participants, or publications); do not infer company-specific percentages from incomplete disclosures. Avoid simplistic funding scores, vote counting, causal claims from subgroup significance or claims of a completely unbiased group. Explain confounding and sparse evidence. Keep current established preventive care and safety safeguards intact.

## 4. Required independently authored audit record
For each source or claim maintain: stable source/claim ID; neutral question; original source URLs and locator; bibliographic/version verification; primary data extraction and verification status; funding categories, entities, evidence locators, direct/indirect ties and sponsor-role unknowns; methodological risk-of-bias rationale; stage applicability and harms; independent conclusion plus certainty/limitations; independent reviewer/model and timestamp; prior-summary exposure status; later comparison status and discrepancies; outstanding actions; qualified reviewer status and publication eligibility. Unknown values remain explicit. Store the independent appraisal separately from archived AI output. A source not fully audited is labeled pending, not silently promoted. Track all records in a review ledger; distinguish not reviewed, source-verified, independently appraised, compared and clinically reviewed. These are separate states.

## 5. Application integration
Claude owns architecture and implementation. Build private prototypes with synthetic fixtures or clearly marked research-only records. Never feed archived AI conclusions into family-facing content, routine generation, medical advice, prioritization, recommendations or decision logic. Build a source-level provenance and funding comparison interface with direct original links, transparent unknowns, limitations, conflicts, and contrary findings. Only independently verified and appropriately reviewed material may be considered for user-facing content, subject to all medical safety, privacy, licensing, and owner release gates. Do not deploy or create recurring monitoring without owner approval.

## 6. Open discovery addition — 2026-11-27
Apply [open-source discovery and bias appraisal standard](OPEN_SOURCE_DISCOVERY_STANDARD.md) to every new topic and claim. Include grey literature and unconventional media as appropriate evidence or leads by source type; independent does not mean bias-free. Do not treat anecdotes, opinions, marketing or spontaneous reports as causal effectiveness or safety evidence. No existing holds removed.
