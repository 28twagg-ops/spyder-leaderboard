# Findings that change what the app should say

| Question | Finding and magnitude | Interpretation for the reader |
|---|---|---|
| When does folic acid matter? | In the MRC recurrence trial, the relative risk was 0.28 (95% CI 0.12–0.71). The participants had a prior affected pregnancy. [MRC report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1677062/) | For average-risk people who could become pregnant, the US recommendation is 400–800 mcg daily starting at least one month before conception through the first 2–3 months. Higher-risk dosing requires individual review. [USPSTF](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/folic-acid-for-the-prevention-of-neural-tube-defects-preventive-medication) |
| Does a cocoa butter cream prevent striae? | Two double-blind trials reported 45.1% vs 48.8%, and 44% vs 55% incidence. Neither excluded no difference. [Osman](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18715434/) · [Buchanan](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijgo.2009.08.008) | A moisturizer can be a comfort purchase. Evidence does not establish reliable prevention. Small combination-product trials cannot identify the contribution of each ingredient. |
| Does collagen preserve pregnancy skin elasticity? | The 2025 synthesis of adult skin-aging trials found favorable pooled results but no detectable benefit in the subgroup without pharmaceutical-company funding. [Review](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.amjmed.2025.04.034) | Skin hydration or wrinkle measures in adults cannot establish striae prevention or fetal safety. Study quality and funding may covary. |
| Can abdominal strength improve with an unchanged gap? | Among 70 people 6–12 months postpartum, 12 weeks of home exercise increased strength by 9 Nm (95% CI 3–16). Resting inter-rectus distance differed by 1 mm (−1 to 4). [Trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37286390/) | Ask what everyday task the person wants to regain. Ultrasound gap and muscle strength measure different things. |
| Who benefits from pelvic floor training? | Initially continent participants had less leakage in late pregnancy, RR 0.38 (0.20–0.72), and at >3–6 months postpartum, RR 0.71 (0.54–0.95). Effects on established leakage were less certain. [Review](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007471.pub4) | Match advice to symptoms and ability to relax as well as contract. |
| Can labor support or perineal care help? | Continuous support was associated with more spontaneous vaginal births, RR 1.08 (1.04–1.12), low certainty. Intrapartum warm compresses probably reduced severe tears, RR 0.46 (0.27–0.79). [Support](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD003766.pub6) · [Perineal techniques](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD016148) | Offer choices. A lower event rate does not guarantee a particular delivery. |
| Should probiotics be routine for gestational diabetes prevention? | The targeted review found uncertain GDM benefit and a preeclampsia signal, RR 1.85 (1.04–3.29). A broader review estimated preeclampsia RR 1.14 (0.84–1.53). [Targeted review](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD009951.pub3) · [Broader review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38447676/) | Avoid a blanket supplement recommendation. Reconcile eligibility, formulations and overlapping trials before considering any combined interpretation. |
| Does omega-3 supplementation prevent early preterm birth? | A review estimated RR 0.58 (0.44–0.77) before 34 weeks. The later ORIP trial found adjusted RR 1.13 (0.79–1.63). [Review](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD003402.pub3) · [ORIP](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1816832) | Distinguish trial dates, baseline diet and supplemental products from food-based fish advice. |
| Does periodontal treatment prevent preterm birth? | In a large pregnancy trial, oral health improved and the preterm-delivery HR was 0.93 (0.63–1.37). [Trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17079762/) | Oral health is sufficient reason to seek needed dental care. A delivery benefit has not been established. |
| Is preventive counseling worthwhile? | Seventeen randomized trials, 3,094 participants, pooled RR 0.61 (0.47–0.78) for onset of perinatal depression. [Evidence review](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/document/evidence-summary/perinatal-depression-preventive-interventions) | The recommendation applies to people at increased risk. Current symptoms need assessment and a treatment pathway. |
