**Remaining-work codes:** R = map canonical article/claim/outcome IDs and check applicability/overlap; E = independently appraise source support, windows, funding/COI, uncertainty and safety; W = write or refine Mother/Baby opening, stage-specific features, shared links and substantive leaf text; A = appropriate original/rights-cleared artwork, alt text and citation; I = integrate routes/state/accessibility and shared content; T = exact-commit automated build/tests; V = rendered mobile/browser review; O = owner acceptance; C = qualified clinical review/publication gate. All codes remain open unless actual evidence is recorded. “Base” means source timeline and phase article pool exist, not a completed week. “Pilot draft” means implemented candidate with unresolved R/E/W/A/T/V/O/C; I is candidate only until tested. No clinical release for any row.
