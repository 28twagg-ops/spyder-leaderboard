# Intervention encyclopedia — research drafts

Each option is linked to its original research/guidance. No new effect estimate or individualized recommendation. Cost is unknown unless independently verified. Context references are not necessarily direct evidence for isolated interventions.

## INT-001 — Moisturizer
**Purpose:** comfort; Dryness.

Moisturizing may improve comfort; prevention of dermal striae is not established.

Sources: [S001](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD000066_topical-preparations-preventing-stretch-marks-pregnancy), [S216](https://www.aad.org/public/cosmetic/scars-stretch-marks/stretch-marks-why-appear). Setting: home product. Effort: routine as tolerated. Cost: not verified. See SKIN-001 safety context in JSON.

## INT-002 — Cocoa butter
**Purpose:** prevention; New striae.

Two vehicle-controlled trials did not establish a reliable reduction.

Sources: [S004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18715434/), [S008](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0020729209004366). Setting: home product. Effort: daily until delivery in trials. Cost: not verified. See SKIN-001 safety context in JSON.

## INT-003 — Olive oil
