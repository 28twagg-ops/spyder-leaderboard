# Traditional therapies and elective devices

**A plausible mechanism is only a starting point. Ask what was measured, at which stage, and what harm was checked.**

**Safety by stage.** No home procedure instructions. FDA reports serious injuries with certain RF microneedling uses. Specialist review is needed after healing and during lactation; regulatory clearance depends on device and indication.

## Practical choice

For breech presentation discuss options with the obstetric team. Keep elective devices, injections and investigational procedures out of routine pregnancy plans.

## Effort and cost

Clinical time, device model, indication and follow-up differ substantially. No defensible common price for these interventions.

## What the research measured

Moxibustion studies assessed fetal position, version and birth route. A modest change in presentation does not guarantee fewer cesareans; adverse-event capture is sparse. TENS trials address labor pain, a separate use from cosmetic electrical stimulation. RF, lasers, ultrasound, PRP and red light differ in tissue interaction and exposure.

Moderate certainty for some breech presentation outcomes, uncertain harms. Pregnancy-specific efficacy and safety for the listed elective cosmetic indications remain insufficient in this set. Case series of medically necessary laser care have limited applicability.

- **Randomized trial** · First pregnancy with breech presentation in late gestation. Moxibustion plus usual care vs usual care. Cephalic at birth: 98/130 vs 81/130. External cephalic version after allocation occurred in 1 vs 24; interpretation depends on subsequent care. [S165](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/188144)
- **Single-blind placebo-controlled RCT** · Breech at 33+4 to 35+4 weeks. Moxibustion and acupuncture vs inactive laser. Breech at 37+2 weeks: 72.0% vs 63.4%. Different comparator, combined therapy and earlier endpoint than S165. [S166](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24901279/)
- **Randomized trial** · Breech after week 33, stratified by parity. Moxibustion vs no moxibustion. No clear evidence of improved version in either parity group. Parity strata must not be misread as treatment arms. [S168](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26836801/)

## Funding and disclosures

- Authors reported no funding.  [S168](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26836801/)

Missing disclosures are unknown. Interpret sponsor comparisons within the same indication, design and outcome.

## Educational illustration

Show fetal position at birth, cesarean delivery and adverse-event reporting as three separate outcomes. Use a data-gap marker for devices lacking pregnancy evidence.

## Next extraction

Extract device-specific energy, indication, adverse events and skin type in appropriate settings. Review PRP, peptides, light, ultrasound and late surgery separately.
