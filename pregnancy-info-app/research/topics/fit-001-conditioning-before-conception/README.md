# Conditioning before conception

**Build an activity routine you can sustain. A weight-loss target is a poor proxy for a healthy birth.**

**Safety by stage.** Before conception, avoid imposing trial weight-loss programs on general users. Once pregnant use pregnancy-specific exercise guidance.

## Practical choice

Use the long preparation window for enjoyable aerobic and strength activity adapted to current ability. Bring menstrual changes or fertility concerns to a clinician.

## Effort and cost

The FIT-PLESE intervention lasted 16 weeks and included supplies and medication. Trial burden exceeds ordinary walking.

## What the research measured

Time-to-pregnancy cohorts can be confounded by baseline health, body size and changes in behavior once trying. FIT-PLESE tested a multicomponent program before fertility treatment in people with obesity and unexplained infertility. Metabolic improvements did not establish improved healthy live birth.

Low for an exact exercise prescription that improves fecundability. Direct trial evidence is specific to an infertility population and an active comparator.

- **Open-label multicenter RCT** · BMI ≥30 and unexplained infertility, nine US centers. 16-week intensive weight-loss program vs increased activity before standardized infertility therapy. Healthy live birth in 23/188 vs 29/191. Intervention bundled diet, activity and medication. Primary analysis by allocation. [S050](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8765626/)
