# Breast changes, support and feeding choices

**The available evidence gives no sound cosmetic reason to change a feeding decision.**

**Safety by stage.** Support may be chosen across stages for comfort. A cosmetic procedure during recovery or lactation needs specialist assessment. Do not frame formula, breastfeeding or combined feeding as a cosmetic intervention.

## Practical choice

Choose comfortable support for activity and a feeding approach that fits the family. Discuss future lactation goals before considering reduction surgery.

## Effort and cost

Support garment costs vary by size and access. No preventive cosmetic regimen was established.

## What the research measured

In a retrospective surgical consultation sample, age, pregnancy count, smoking and baseline breast size correlated with ptosis. Feeding history was not an independent predictor. Referral selection, recall, confounding and limited size preclude a universal causal estimate. Surgical preservation of tissue connecting the nipple and chest wall may affect lactation.

Low for causal effects on appearance. Surgical literature is observational with varied definitions of feeding success. Sports bra studies concern movement and discomfort.

- **Retrospective surgical consultation sample** · Previously pregnant people seeking breast aesthetic surgery. History of breastfeeding vs none. Breastfeeding was not independently associated with ptosis. Fifty-four reported breastfeeding. Selected surgical population, recall, possible residual confounding.
