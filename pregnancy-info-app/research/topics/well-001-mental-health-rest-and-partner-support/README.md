# Mental health, rest and partner support

**Ask for practical help early. Counseling can lower depression risk for people at increased risk.**

**Safety by stage.** During pregnancy and the year after delivery, thoughts of harming oneself or the baby need immediate help. Severe confusion or loss of contact with reality requires emergency assessment. Postpartum follow-up is an ongoing process.

## Practical choice

Choose who can protect rest time, bring meals, take calls or help with appointments. Ask about preventive counseling if at increased risk. Concerning mood symptoms warrant assessment now.

## Effort and cost

Counseling requires appointments; telephone peer support is a different intervention. No transferable fee per participant verified.

## What the research measured

Trials of cognitive behavioral and interpersonal counseling measured onset of perinatal depression. Peer-support trials often measured symptom-score thresholds. These differ from a confirmed diagnosis or treatment of existing depression. Sleep opportunity, actual sleep and biological sleep disruption are distinct.

Moderate support for preventive counseling in higher-risk populations. Peer support is promising for defined settings. Body-image and sleep evidence varies by intervention, timing and measure.

- **Multisite randomized trial** · Postpartum people at elevated depression risk. Telephone peer support vs usual care. EPDS score >12 at 12 weeks: 40/297 vs 78/315. Difference unclear at 24 weeks. 612 assessed at 12 weeks. Screening threshold is not a diagnostic interview. [S150](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19147637/)
