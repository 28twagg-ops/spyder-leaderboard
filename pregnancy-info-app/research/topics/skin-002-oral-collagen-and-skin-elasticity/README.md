# Oral collagen and skin elasticity

**Adult skin studies give conflicting results. Pregnancy stretch-mark prevention remains unproven.**

**Safety by stage.** Before conception, adult cosmetic findings apply only to similar populations. During pregnancy and lactation, product-specific safety and benefit for this indication remain unestablished.

## Practical choice

A collagen supplement has no established place on a pregnancy prevention checklist. Review any supplement with the prenatal clinician.

## Effort and cost

Eight weeks in T014. No pregnancy-specific benefit or price-effectiveness estimate.

## What the research measured

Hydrolyzed peptides have been tested using instruments that measure hydration or skin deformation. A short change in forearm elasticity does not establish prevention of abdominal striae, improved function or fetal safety. Cell-culture findings concern matrix production only.

Very low for pregnancy prevention due to indirectness. A 23-trial adult synthesis found favorable pooled results, but benefits did not persist in the stratum without pharmaceutical-company funding. The subgroup is observational and may also reflect study quality or product differences.

- **Double-blind RCT** · Women aged 35–55, cosmetic skin-aging study. Branded bovine collagen peptides vs placebo, eight weeks. Investigators reported improved hydration, elasticity and eye wrinkles. Pregnancy and striae outcomes absent. Separate fibroblast experiment. [S014](https://doi.org/10.3390%2Fcosmetics12020079)

## Funding and disclosures

- GELITA supplied the collagen product. An author reported industry-supported lectures and collagen-related patents. [S014](https://doi.org/10.3390%2Fcosmetics12020079)

Missing disclosures are unknown. Interpret sponsor comparisons within the same indication, design and outcome.
