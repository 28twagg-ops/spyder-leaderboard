# Planning, fertility, medication and vaccines

**The highest-value early step is reviewing health, medicines and vaccines before trying.**

**Safety by stage.** Do not stop needed medicines independently. Some vaccines belong before conception, others during pregnancy. Local schedules, products and seasonal timing need current clinical review.

## Practical choice

Bring medication and supplement lists, vaccination history and family history. Include partner concerns. Optional urine ovulation tests may help some couples. Discuss evaluation sooner for known fertility risks.

## Effort and cost

Optional US retail example: 10 digital ovulation tests listed at $29.99 ($3.00/test) before tax or shipping. The number used per cycle varies. [Retail listing](https://www.walgreens.com/q/ovulation-tests)

## What the research measured

Review chronic conditions before conception so time-dependent changes can be planned safely. Thyroid testing and treatment depend on clinical history and results. Urinary hormone tests can identify fertile days; fertility-awareness methods have different evidence. Paternal research includes observational and mechanistic evidence with limited intervention trials.

Guideline-based preconception care. Moderate certainty for the specific urinary-test population, with substantial manufacturer sponsorship. Fertility probabilities vary with age and duration of trying.

- **Follow-up of home-based randomized trial** · People aged 18–40 attempting conception. Connected urine hormone testing vs no test over up to two cycles. Reported cumulative live birth 24.5% vs 17.5%. Pregnancy outcomes available for 198 of 247 reported pregnancies. Commercial involvement and missing outcomes matter. [S219](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8812496/)

## Funding and disclosures

- Review identified manufacturer funding for many underlying ovulation-test studies.  [S146](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD011345_timed-intercourse-couples-trying-conceive)
- SPD Development Company Limited, a subsidiary of SPD Swiss Precision Diagnostics. The follow-up report identifies four authors as company employees. [S219](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8812496/)

Missing disclosures are unknown. Interpret sponsor comparisons within the same indication, design and outcome.
