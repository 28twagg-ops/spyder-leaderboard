# Diastasis, strength and abdominal function

**Strength can improve even when the measured gap stays the same.**

**Safety by stage.** The exercise trial does not establish immediate postpartum readiness. Wound problems, pain and pelvic heaviness warrant individual assessment.

## Practical choice

Discuss graded strengthening for everyday tasks. Use symptoms and function alongside any ultrasound measurement.

## Effort and cost

T028 used five home sessions per week for 12 weeks. Session duration was not extracted.

## What the research measured

The linea alba joins the two rectus muscles. Ultrasound gap depends on position, measurement site and contraction. Torque, muscle thickness, pain and task performance answer separate questions. In a 12-week trial starting 6–12 months postpartum, curl-ups improved strength without a clear change in resting separation.

Moderate for strength in that specific trial population. Lower certainty about an optimal program or durable improvement in pain. Within-group changes during early recovery can include natural healing.

- **Assessor-blinded RCT** · Diastasis, 6–12 months postpartum. Home abdominal exercises five days/week for 12 weeks vs no program. Strength increased by 9 Nm; resting gap difference was 1 mm. Gap assessed 2 cm above umbilicus. One protocol and population. [S028](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37286390/)
