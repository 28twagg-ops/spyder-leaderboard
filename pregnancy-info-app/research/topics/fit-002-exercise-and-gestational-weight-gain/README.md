# Exercise and gestational weight gain

**For uncomplicated pregnancies, regular activity can continue with adjustments. Weight-gain goals depend on the pregnancy.**

**Safety by stage.** Across trimesters adapt for comfort, balance and medical conditions. Stop and seek care for bleeding, painful contractions or fluid leakage. No calorie-deficit goal.

## Practical choice

Discuss an activity plan. ACOG uses at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity per week in uncomplicated pregnancies. Use pre-pregnancy BMI and singleton or twin status with the care team for weight guidance.

## Effort and cost

Guideline target for eligible uncomplicated pregnancy is 150 minutes/week of moderate aerobic activity. Walking can fit this without specialized equipment.

## What the research measured

Trials of antenatal lifestyle programs used different combinations of diet, exercise and counseling. Mean gestational gain was modestly lower. This is a group comparison, not a reason to subtract kilograms from a personal target. Multiple pregnancy and medical complications change applicability.

Moderate for a modest mean reduction in gain. Pooling different programs limits attribution to exercise alone. Data in high-risk groups require separate assessment.
