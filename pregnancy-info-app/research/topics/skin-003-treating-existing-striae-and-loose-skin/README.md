# Treating existing striae and loose skin

**Mature stretch marks may change with treatment. Trials rarely measure how much improvement matters to the patient.**

**Safety by stage.** Defer elective procedures during pregnancy. Later assessment must account for healing, site, skin type and lactation. RF microneedling has a device-specific FDA harm warning.

## Practical choice

If marks remain bothersome after recovery, a dermatologist can discuss observation and procedure tradeoffs, including changes in pigmentation.

## Effort and cost

Procedures involve appointments and follow-up. Local prices and retreatment frequency need verification.

## What the research measured

Trials distinguish red, newer marks from pale, established marks. Some compare treated sides of one person. This controls some individual differences but does not provide an untreated comparison or independent observations for each side. Excess skin is a different outcome.

Low for comparative cosmetic benefit. Small active-comparator trials, variable phototypes, follow-up and physician rating scales limit estimates.

- **Paired split-body study** · People with existing striae. Microneedling plus ascorbic acid vs fractional CO2 laser plus ascorbic acid. Physician ratings and patient satisfaction favored the laser side. Twenty-two people, not 44 independent participants. No untreated side. [S019](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/dth/2453160)
