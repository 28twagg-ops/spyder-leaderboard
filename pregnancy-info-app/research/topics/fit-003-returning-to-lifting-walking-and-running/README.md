# Returning to lifting, walking and running

**Recovery follows symptoms and healing. A date alone cannot clear someone to run.**

**Safety by stage.** Immediate recovery after uncomplicated vaginal birth differs from major tearing, assisted birth, cesarean or medical complications. Progress individually in every pathway.

## Practical choice

Start with tolerable movement, then build duration and load. Seek pelvic-health or obstetric input for pain, leakage, heaviness, bleeding or surgical concerns.

## Effort and cost

The Canadian first-year guideline uses a gradual, individualized progression toward 120 minutes/week of moderate-to-vigorous activity over four or more days. Early healing and contraindications take precedence.

## What the research measured

The Canadian first-year guideline distinguishes early gentle movement from a later weekly activity target. Running consensus papers synthesize clinician opinion on readiness and gradual loading. A consensus test is not a validated prediction of injury.

Supported for gradual activity after birth. Low or unassessed for specific readiness tests, exact lifting limits and universal return dates.

- **Randomized exercise trial** · Exclusively breastfeeding at 6–8 weeks postpartum. Structured aerobic training vs control over 12 weeks. Fitness improved without a detectable difference in milk output, composition or infant weight gain. Small selected group; cannot exclude rare harms or generalize to restricted energy intake. [S221](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8289849/)
