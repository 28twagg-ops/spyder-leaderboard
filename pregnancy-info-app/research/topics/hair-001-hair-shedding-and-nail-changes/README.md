# Hair shedding and nail changes

**Shedding often peaks around four months after birth and usually improves over the first year.**

**Safety by stage.** Medication decisions differ in pregnancy and lactation. Minoxidil needs a product- and infant-specific assessment. Typical postpartum shedding does not establish deficiency.

## Practical choice

Use gentle hair care if desired. Seek dermatology advice for patchy loss, scalp symptoms, persistent loss or failure to regain fullness by about a year.

## Effort and cost

Observation is an option for typical shedding. Evaluating persistent symptoms involves a clinical visit.

## What the research measured

A shift in the proportion of follicles in the resting phase can cause diffuse shedding months after birth. Timing varies. Self-selected clinic series can reveal underlying traction or pattern hair loss but cannot estimate the prevalence in all parents. Nail texture and growth can also change.

Supported for the broad natural history. Precise incidence, predictors and supplement benefit are uncertain. Questionnaire studies have recall and selection bias.

- **Observational hair-cycle comparison** · Pregnancy and postpartum timepoint groups. Sixth and ninth months of pregnancy vs fourth month postpartum. Higher fraction of resting hairs at four months postpartum. Hair-cycle measurement differs from diagnosis of permanent hair loss. [S111](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23682615/)
