# Contraception and pregnancy preparation — preliminary independent-source research addition
Date: 2026-11-27. Research only; clinical release disabled. Owner explicitly requested addition. Source inventory and actual claim-linked app content are stored in [contraception.json](../../app/src/data/contraception.json); this is an additive packet, not a change to the existing 770-source canonical count. 20 source records, 11 focused articles, 15 annual typical-use rows; publications, reviews and guidelines are not independent cohorts.

## Scope and discovery
Population: people of reproductive age preventing pregnancy now or planning future pregnancy, including preconception, postpartum/breastfeeding and partner methods. Comparators: no contraceptive hormones, LNG IUD, combined hormones, systemic progestin-only, behavioral/barrier methods. Outcomes: typical-use first-year pregnancy, exposure (specific analyte only), bleeding, discontinuation, VTE, mood, weight, cancer, return to fertility, procedural harms, emergency use, accessibility. US guidance scope. Searches 2026-11-27 in public web indexes of PubMed, PMC, CDC, Cochrane, original journals and NCI: contraceptive methods typical use; US MEC/SPR; LNG copper randomized trials/meta-analysis; LNG pharmacokinetics; pregravid contraception fecundability; fertility-awareness systematic review; depression randomized versus registry; venous thromboembolism contemporary cohort; breast cancer; weight; IUD perforation; emergency LNG versus copper trial. Prior project reproduction topic consulted for scope only, not used as evidence. Not an exhaustive database search; no PRISMA count invented. Included directly relevant original publication records and public guidance; excluded duplicate abstract/full-text versions and manufacturer marketing. Underlying studies not individually deduplicated; no vote-counting or pooled new effect estimates.

## Appraisal
Every record has source locator, access status, funding, COI, design-specific limitations and explicit incomplete fields. Full-text verified where accessible (Rowe, CDC, Girum); indexed original abstracts and disclosures used where publisher/PMC blocked. CT-04 is WHO/UN funding; CT-06 has Bayer affiliations, funding/control not fully verified; CT-15 reports Bayer funding. CT-07 has public funding indicators plus in-kind/departmental ties. CT-13 charity/public funding; CT-12 insurer grant and author financial ties; CT-10 no specific grant reported. Do not label unknown disclosures independent or interpret sponsorship as automatic invalidity. CT-05 overlaps primary trials including possible CT-04; CT-06 overlaps prior model publication; CT-10 and CT-11 compare distinct populations/endpoints; CT-12 related Danish registry studies may overlap. No count of independent replications inferred. Formal RoB2/ROBINS-I/AMSTAR2 and GRADE pending. Numeric clinical-study estimates retained as source observations only, not used to rank methods. Only CDC typical-use estimates plotted, with source, year, denominator and limitations. No dosage schedules, fertility predictor, method selection algorithm or universal washout. Public pages are educational prototype only; individual review still required.
