## GA-001 — ABD-001

**Limitations:** Considerable heterogeneity, pooled sample not available from abstract, full text paywalled. Not proof that all exercise is ineffective; anatomical distance is distinct from strength, function or symptoms.

## GA-002 — ABD-001
27 RCTs, 1340 participants, 39 interventions; 26 studies in main network. 42 pairwise comparisons, only 2 replicated in more than one study. 17 low risk, 8 high risk, 2 unclear per authors. Highest ranked TCMEB+KT estimate had low confidence; very early initiation raises natural-recovery concern. Other selected combinations rated higher confidence by authors.

**Limitations:** Indirect comparisons and sparse replication; no independent pooled comparison with Lyons; different intervention definitions and timing; not a safety or functional outcome ranking. SUCRA is a ranking metric, not probability of individual success. Authors could not search EMBASE, CINAHL, PEDro. No claim that individual components independently work.

[R016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41224804/). Locator: Publisher abstract, Results, Strengths and limitations, Funding, Competing interest.

## GA-005 — SKIN-001
Overall incidence 37.6% vs 33.3% treated/control, not significant. No prior striae subgroup 5.6% vs 35%, reported OR 9.2 (95% CI 1.0–83.3), p=.031; direction corresponds to higher odds in control. ## GA-006 — SKIN-003
78 healthy participants at least 6 months postpartum; 25 used branded topical cream, 28 hydrating control, 25 hydrating cream plus oral Centella. Six weeks; authors report better instrument and appearance outcomes with combination and no observed side effects.

**Limitations:** Comparative registry, randomization/blinding not established, multiple outcomes, absolute effect sizes unavailable, funding/COI and lactation status unknown. No general safety or causal benefit. EMA cutaneous monograph section 4.6 advises against covered medicinal use in pregnancy/lactation due to inadequate data; does not directly establish oral risk.

[G001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30293400/), [G002](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/herbal-monograph/european-union-herbal-monograph-centella-asiatica-l-urb-herba-revision-1_en.pdf). Locator: PubMed and publisher abstract; EMA section 4.6 p6 (image inspected).
