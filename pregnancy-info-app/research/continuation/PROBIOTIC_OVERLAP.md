# NUTR-002 — Probiotics and preeclampsia: review overlap

## Quick takeaway
Probiotic supplements have not reliably prevented gestational diabetes. Some trials raise a preeclampsia safety concern; broader research leaves uncertainty. Do not make concentrated probiotics a routine pregnancy-prevention task. Ordinary fermented foods are a different exposure.

## What changed
The earlier evidence map identified conflicting estimates but had not matched the trials. The 2021 Cochrane prevention review (S094) analysis 1.9, printed page 60, gives four independent trial comparisons. All point estimates favor placebo for this adverse outcome; none individually has a 95% interval excluding 1. The pooled fixed-effect estimate is RR 1.85 (1.04–3.29), 955 women; the review rated certainty high for this endpoint. The total is 31/472 versus 17/483, descriptive crude rates 6.57% versus 3.52%. Do not substitute the crude ratio for the published Mantel-Haenszel pooled effect.

## Overlap map (preeclampsia endpoint only)
Callaway 2019, Lindsay 2014 and Pellonperä 2019 occur in both figures; HUMBA is in the Cochrane figure but not the broader figure for this endpoint. It occurs elsewhere in the broader review. Thus at least three shared cohorts, not 15 independent trials. The union of the labeled PE comparison sets is provisionally 12 cohorts (4+11−3); this is not a new meta-analysis, and identity of additional broader studies still needs confirmation. Do not combine the two pooled estimates or count them as separate trials. Request author clarification about HUMBA endpoint selection and differences in eligibility and outcome data.
