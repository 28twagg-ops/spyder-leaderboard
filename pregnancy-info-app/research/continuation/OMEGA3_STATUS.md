# NUTR-001 — Omega-3: chronology and baseline status

## Quick takeaway
Adequate dietary omega-3 matters. Trials do not show the same benefit for everyone; higher-dose supplementation for preterm birth is a clinician discussion, especially for low baseline intake/status. Do not treat this as a cosmetic intervention.

| Evidence | Comparison | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| S083 Cochrane 2018 | Omega-3 vs no omega-3 | Birth <34 weeks RR 0.58 (0.44–0.77) |
| S082 ORIP 2019 | Supplement vs control | Birth <34 weeks adjusted RR 1.13 (0.79–1.63); 61/2734 vs 55/2752 |
| ORIP 2020 secondary analysis | Low total omega-3 status ≤4.1% of total fatty acids | 0.73% vs 3.16%; RR 0.23 (0.07–0.79) |
| ORIP 2023 exploratory analysis | Low status subgroup | OR 0.30 (0.10–0.93), not a risk ratio |
| ADORE 2021 independent trial | 1000 mg vs 200 mg DHA, not placebo | 9/540 vs 12/492 early preterm births; Bayesian posterior probability 0.81 for primary analysis |
| ADORE low-DHA subgroup | Same trial and comparator | 5/249 vs 9/219; posterior probability 0.93 |

Sources: [2018 review](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD003402.pub3), [ORIP](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1816832), [2020 exploratory analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32034969/), [2023 exploratory analysis](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/13/4/e070220), [ADORE](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34308309/).

ORIP was reported after the earlier review search; its subsequent analyses reuse ORIP participants and cannot be treated as independent replications. The 2023 paper explicitly notes multiple comparisons were not adjusted. ADORE is a distinct trial with a low-dose active comparator and Bayesian analysis; do not present posterior probability as a frequentist confidence interval or individual chance of success. The biomarkers/thresholds are not interchangeable. The 2023 ORIP article reports an investigator patent related to fatty-acid measurement and supply relationships; original ORIP funding and donated capsules are recorded in baseline NUTR-001. Extract ADORE disclosures from the full report before any funding subgroup analysis.

A 2024 clinical practice guideline differentiates baseline intake/status when discussing higher supplemental doses; NIH ODS summarizes it alongside broader seafood advice. [Guideline](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajogmf.2023.101251), [NIH pregnancy fact sheet](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Pregnancy-HealthProfessional/). This is not a universal dose instruction, and food advice is distinct from supplementation. Use guideline jurisdiction, clinical review and individual dietary context.

## Chart specification
Separate review, all-comers trial and exploratory subgroup lanes. Do not mix RR, adjusted RR, OR and posterior probability as if equivalent. Never sum ORIP subgroups as independent populations. ADORE arm counts can be plotted as observed event rates with exact denominators; its trial probability is separate. No new pooled analysis was calculated.
