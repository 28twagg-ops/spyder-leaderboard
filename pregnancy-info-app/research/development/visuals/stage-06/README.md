## Visual safety and implementation checklist
1. Prefer existing scientific images and primary data charts, not AI-generated anatomical depictions. No visual files generated or imported in Stage 6.
2. Resolve exact image/figure identity, permissions, license version, rightsholder, attribution and whether modifications are permitted; capture version/date and source hash after lawful acquisition. Confirm model or photograph consent/performer restrictions. No implicit endorsement.
3. Qualified clinician/anatomy reviewer checks labels, developmental timing, arrows and clinical context. Verify obstetric dating vs age after fertilization. Preserve range and uncertainty rather than deterministic week labels. Avoid diagnostic illustrations used as self-diagnosis.
4. Verify charts against original numeric estimates, units, population, study group, confidence intervals and corrections/retractions. Do not extract held evidence; label schematic and clinical reference differently. Repeated figure endpoints do not count as independent trials.
5. Review full videos, captions/transcripts and age/condition-specific safety. Distinguish parent education from clinician-only procedures. Provide concise alt text, readable contrast, mobile-friendly rendering and accurate cross-references; no third-party media reuse without permission.
6. Link each asset to actual canonical topic/article ID after app integration review, not guessed destination IDs; preserve all prior clinical holds. Each record currently has clinical_review=false, publish=false, automation_allowed=false, asset_downloaded=false, reuse_approved=false.
