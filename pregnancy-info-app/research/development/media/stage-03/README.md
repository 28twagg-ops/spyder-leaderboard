## Owner direction: international-first discovery

Prioritize international and local-language scientific and media discovery, publicly funded and independently replicated work, regional universities and research groups, and culturally grounded knowledge. Search international evidence at least as intensively as US evidence; do not predetermine findings or grade research by geography. Apply the same funding, conflicts, methodology, safety and outcome-specific certainty standards to all sources. Keep US clinical guidance and jurisdiction-specific applicability for users in the United States; contrast with international guidance where meaningful. Label unknown funding as unknown. Cochrane Handbook chapter 7 (https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-07) and chapter 15 (https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-15) provide methodological support for separate conflict and applicability appraisal. Do not infer independence from institutional affiliation, country or noncommercial branding.
