## Geographic parity objective
For each major Everwell topic, search at least as intensively internationally as in the US. Track eligible primary report counts and independent study counts separately by location; aim for at least as many eligible non-US primary studies collectively as US-only studies where available. Do not force artificial quotas or discard stronger evidence. Treat mixed-country trials as a separate category; avoid counting one trial repeatedly. Compare populations, dietary fortification, clinical practice, parity, baseline nutrition, health access, outcome definitions and currency of guidance before applying to US users. No extrapolation from non-US or US evidence without assessing applicability.

Chinese and Persian database coverage documented via included reviews, not direct searches.
