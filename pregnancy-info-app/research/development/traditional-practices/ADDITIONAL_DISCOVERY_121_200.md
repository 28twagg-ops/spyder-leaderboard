## Source ledger
- A: [Malawi xylitol cluster trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39541971/). Reported fewer preterm and low-birthweight deliveries; setting, cluster design and product supply must be examined; not proof that all gum prevents preterm birth. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- B: [Cochrane periodontal treatment](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD005297_treating-gum-disease-prevent-adverse-birth-outcomes-pregnant-women). Uncertain preterm birth effect; dental care remains valuable for oral health. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- C: [Pregnancy cranberry review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38022216/). Feasibility and limited pregnancy studies; not treatment of symptomatic UTI, bacteriuria or replacement for antibiotics. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- D: [Cochrane probiotics for gestational diabetes](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD009951_probiotics-prevent-gestational-diabetes-mellitus). Uncertain GDM prevention; review reports increased preeclampsia risk, check exact estimates and current evidence. No supplement regimen. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- E: [Cochrane heartburn in pregnancy](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD011379_interventions-heartburn-pregnancy). Common lifestyle measures discussed; not direct randomized evidence for each separate habit. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- F: [Office on Women's Health pregnancy discomforts](https://womenshealth.gov/pregnancy/youre-pregnant-now-what/body-changes-and-discomforts). Splint fitting guidance; not comparative trial evidence. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- G: [AGA hemorrhoid expert update](https://gastro.org/clinical-guidance/diagnosis-and-treatment-of-hemorrhoids/). Conservative treatment; limited sitz bath evidence. Do not assume bleeding is hemorrhoids. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- H: [Pregnancy restless legs review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39038317/). Nonpharmacologic review lead; modality-specific results not extracted; distinguish RLS from cramps and assess iron status clinically. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- I: [Cochrane labor analgesia overview](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD009234_pain-management-women-labour-overview). Older overview, insufficient evidence for hypnosis, TENS, aromatherapy, biofeedback and sterile water injections at time of review. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- J: [NICE intrapartum care](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng235/chapter/Recommendations). Current indexed guidance discusses TENS preferences and sterile water injections for back pain; do not offer hypnosis/acupuncture/acupressure routinely. No injection instructions. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- K: [Complementary therapies review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39685103/). Pain relief signals for reflexology and VR; extract direct trial comparisons and heterogeneity. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- L: [Aromatherapy labor umbrella review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41827525/). Possible pain relief; review overlap, product variability and odor blinding; no ingestion or exposure protocol. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- M: [Cochrane relaxation techniques](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD009514_fangsongjishuhuanjiefenmiantengtong). Music and other relaxation techniques studied; extract separate outcomes. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- N: [Postpartum lumbopelvic physiotherapy review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12231622/). 37 RCTs, 1739 women; many different interventions, limited long-term evidence. Presence in review does not establish efficacy. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- O: [Sham-controlled postpartum kinesiotaping trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41954431/). Reported improvements in pain and disability; check sample, effects, adverse events, device supply and blinding. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- P: [Cochrane pelvic floor training](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD007471_how-effective-pelvic-floor-muscle-training-undertaken-during-pregnancy-or-after-birth-preventing-or). Antenatal prevention mid-postpartum UI RR 0.71 (0.54–0.95), 5 trials 673 participants. Treatment vs prevention differ; pelvic floor pain and hypertonicity matter. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- Q: [Postpartum exercise systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39694630/). Signals for pelvic floor disorders and inter-rectus distance; distance not synonymous with function or appearance. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- R: [Abdominal exercise vs binding meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40018828/). Effects differ by measurement location; no blanket body-shape guarantee. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- T: [Postpartum sleep network meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40745901/). Very large early postpartum reflexology effect estimate requires scrutiny of small trials, bias and indirectness; ranking not definitive. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- U: [Bedroom environment randomized trials](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3066036/). Limited benefit from combined changes; no attribution to sound machine alone; infant noise safety separate. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- V: [Postpartum aromatherapy review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31246645/). Mixed symptom outcomes; separate anxiety, sleep, pain; exposure safety needs assessment. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- W: [Cochrane nipple pain review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10885851/). Insufficient evidence for lanolin, glycerine dressings, breast milk or all-purpose ointment; distinguish pain and wound healing. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- X: [Moisturizing therapy review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39713981/). Newer review includes hydrogel, lanolin and other approaches; full text for silver-cap subgroup needed; compare W. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- Y: [Nipple pain systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16020410/). Older review covers warm water and tea compresses plus latch education; each product's effect unverified here. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- Z: [Nipple wound review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13130628/). Natural topicals studied; no recommendation for honey or herbal products that might be ingested by infants; infant safety review required. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- AA: [Delayed newborn bathing review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35972992/). Possible reduced hypothermia, low-certainty evidence; timing and observational design matter. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- AB: [WHO postnatal care recommendations](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK579650/). First bath of healthy term newborn delayed at least 24h; routine emollients not recommended for prevention; guidance and underlying studies not independent evidence. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- AC: [Indian Academy of Pediatrics skin care guidance](https://indianpediatrics.net/feb2021/feb-153-161.htm). - AD: [Nepal newborn oil cluster RCT](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31870338/). 995 neonates; sunflower vs mustard oil skin barrier outcomes, not proof all oils safe or effective. Nested within NCT01177111. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- AE: [Infant skin care IPD meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33550675/). Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- AF: [Infant sleep machine sound study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24590753/). 14 devices at different distances; measured sound pressure, not longitudinal hearing loss. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- AG: [AAP excessive noise policy](https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/152/5/e2023063752/194468/Preventing-Excessive-Noise-Exposure-in-Infants). Sound exposure safety, not efficacy for settling. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- AH: [Sleep SAAF responsive parenting RCT](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/pmid/37148966/). Parent-reported soothing measures; intervention bundle not proof each component works. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- AI: [Cochrane non-drug procedural pain review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD006275_how-can-caregivers-manage-pain-infants-and-young-children-undergoing-painful-procedures). 138 RCTs, 11058 participants; promising sucking and facilitated tucking outcomes; age and pain measure distinctions. Funding, conflicts, underlying study deduplication and full risk of bias not audited in this pass.
- AJ: [Cochrane breast milk and procedural pain](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD004950_breastfeeding-or-breast-milk-procedural-pain-newborn-babies).
