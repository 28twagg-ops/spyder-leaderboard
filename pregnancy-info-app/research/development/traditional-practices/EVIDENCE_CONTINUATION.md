## Source ledger

### R01 — [Cochrane timed intercourse 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37709293/)
- Appraisal: FABM live birth RR 0.95 (95% CI 0.76–1.20; 2 trials, 157 people; low certainty); urine hormone tests probably improve pregnancy/live birth in selected population. Method-specific effects cannot be transferred to mucus or temperature alone.
- Funding / interests: Funding not checked; ovulation test company involvement in underlying trials needs audit.
- Access: indexed abstract or summary unless funding details specifically extracted; exact full-text tables, registrations, corrections and underlying trial overlap pending.

### R02 — [Dietary patterns and ART review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9156378/)
- Appraisal: 13 studies, 3638 participants, mostly cohorts; Iranian traditional diet trial combined dietary and other lifestyle changes. Moderate/high bias; inconsistent results.
- Funding / interests: NHMRC fellowship APP1164771; no other external funding reported; authors declare no conflicts; one author affiliated with IVF provider.
- Access: indexed abstract or summary unless funding details specifically extracted; exact full-text tables, registrations, corrections and underlying trial overlap pending.

### R04 — [PRESTO male heat cohort](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35924639/)
- Appraisal: 3041 couples; hot tub/bath frequency ≥3 vs 0/month fecundability ratio 0.87 (0.70–1.07); little association with sauna, laptops, underwear or seat heaters. Self-report, confounding, CI includes null; fecundability not semen measurement.
- Funding / interests: Full text access challenge; funding unknown for this report.
- Access: indexed abstract or summary unless funding details specifically extracted; exact full-text tables, registrations, corrections and underlying trial overlap pending.

### R05 — [Smith et al. IVF acupuncture sham-controlled RCT](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2681194)
- Appraisal: 848 randomized; live births 74/405 vs 72/404 (18.3% vs 17.8%); RD 0.5 percentage points (−4.9 to 5.8), RR 1.02 (0.76–1.38). Null comparison in fresh IVF, not all acupuncture indications. Sham may have nonspecific effects.
- Funding / interests: Australian NHMRC APP1003661; needles donated by Helio Supply; disclosed IVF equity interests, practitioner collaboration, other industry ties; funder reportedly no role.
- Access: indexed abstract or summary unless funding details specifically extracted; exact full-text tables, registrations, corrections and underlying trial overlap pending.
