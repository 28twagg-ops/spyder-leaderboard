## Priority comparative findings for Stage 7
- Chinese postpartum confinement: observational protective signals conflict with a 2023 review (PMID 37903136) of 16 studies; 4 protective, 2 opposite, 10 nonsignificant. Descriptive study counts are not a pooled estimate or evidence grade. No specific funding and no competing interests declared for the review, not necessarily its included studies. Separate voluntary practical support, rest and access to care from restrictive rules; no depression treatment substitution.
- Guerrero, Mexico: PMID 35038990; cluster trial of culturally responsive support and coordination, not individual traditional methods. Composite outcome RD −0.06 (95% CI −0.09 to −0.02); 872 pregnancies, four municipalities, retrospective registration and limited mortality power. Funding and author disclosure recorded. No inference of equivalent home/hospital safety or endorsement of unreviewed procedures.
- Sarlahi, Nepal: PMID 38423547; registry NCT01177111; overall neonatal mortality RR 0.95 (95% CI 0.84–1.08) for sunflower versus mustard oil; no clear benefit, no evidence of equivalence. Compare with earlier hospital preterm studies and related skin-barrier reports; do not double-count parent trial. Funding Gates Foundation and NICHD; full sponsor-role audit pending. No universal newborn oil advice.
- Moroccan Souss Massa ethnopharmacology survey: DOI 10.3389/fphar.2025.1567930; no financial support or commercial/financial conflicts reported by authors. Self-reported use is not evidence of safety or efficacy. Do not use herbal induction, hemorrhage treatment, infant remedies or unvalidated ingestion protocols.
