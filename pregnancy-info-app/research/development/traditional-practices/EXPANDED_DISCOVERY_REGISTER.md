## Scope and status
120 distinct question entries within this register; overlap with earlier Everwell research is expected and has not been reconciled globally. These are not 120 new clinical articles, proven treatments, or fully appraised studies. The original 28 source checks remain intact. 28 source leads screened at abstract, summary or selected full-text-section level; some were already in the archive. Counts refer to entries, not unique trials. Same-agent preliminary review, not independent or clinical review.

Status codes: S = source-linked preliminary question (48); C = cultural/context source, no causal efficacy inference (3); H = safety hold, no instructions or recommendations (11); P = prior packet carryover (4); L = discovery lead only, not individually screened (54). An S label is not a favorable result or evidence grade. Missing evidence does not mean no effect, nor establish safety. For H entries retain neutral research question or safety explanation only. No self-induction protocols, infant herbal remedies, unsafe sleep, restrictive diets or delay of indicated medical care. Use MEDICAL_SAFETY.md and OPEN_SOURCE_DISCOVERY_STANDARD.md.

### Source appraisal and search log
Searches performed 2026-11-27 via web-indexed PubMed, Cochrane, PMC, WHO, publisher and guidance pages: nausea ginger/acupressure/quince/aroma; topical stretch mark prevention; pregnancy pain/leg cramps/constipation; labor massage/positions/water/continuous support; dates/raspberry leaf; traditional postpartum confinement/food taboos; galactagogues and engorgement; infant carrying/massage/skin-to-skin/colic. These are targeted discovery searches, not systematic reviews. Funding and conflicts unknown unless specifically noted below; named university support is self-reported, not a guarantee of independence. Original trial funding, overlap, adverse events, corrections, registrations and full risk-of-bias assessments pending. No new media timestamp, visual rights or production approval claimed.

- **N1** — [Cochrane nausea interventions](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD007575_interventions-nausea-and-vomiting-early-pregnancy). Review of heterogeneous randomized trials; certainty and outcome comparability limited; not proof that each remedy works.
- **N2** — [2026 nausea network meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13118981/). Ginger, quince and acupressure have possible symptom benefit; indirect comparisons and varying scales limit ranking. Semmelweis University research fund; authors report no conflicts and no sponsor role.
- **N3** — [2018 complementary nausea systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30319738/). Identifies cardamom, citrus/mint aroma, ginger and pressure-point studies. Small, heterogeneous trials; odor blinding and individual study quality require checking.
- **H1** — [Herbal safety systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30969204/). Observational associations and adverse-event reports raise safety questions, including almond oil and raspberry leaf; confounding prevents causal inference. No dose or usage recommendation.
- **D1** — [Cochrane constipation review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD011448_interventions-treating-constipation-pregnancy). Few small trials; insufficient evidence for comprehensive comparisons, including safety.
- **D2** — [Cochrane pregnancy leg cramp review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD010655_interventions-leg-cramps-during-pregnancy). Effectiveness and safety of oral mineral/vitamin interventions unclear; non-drug approaches discussed, not established as effective by this review.
- **K1** — [Cochrane stretch mark prevention review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD000066_topical-preparations-preventing-stretch-marks-pregnancy). Does not establish reliable prevention from topical preparations studied; multi-ingredient products prevent attribution to single components.
- **K2** — [Topical stretch mark review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25255817/). Limited signal for Centella and some massage combinations, no demonstrated benefit for cocoa butter or olive oil. Compare against K1 and check ingredient/safety differences.
- **M1** — [Cochrane pregnancy back/pelvic pain review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23904227/). Several approaches showed possible pain relief, but effect sizes and quality varied; possible reporting bias. Earlier review, update required.
- **M2** — [Physiotherapy systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26018758/). Positive appraisal for acupuncture and belts, less certain for exercise interventions; differing outcome and quality methods require reconciliation.
- **B1** — [Cochrane complementary labor evidence index](https://cam.cochrane.org/labour). Historical review index, discovery only; retrieve current dedicated reviews before extracting effects.
- **B2** — [NICE intrapartum guidance summary](https://www.bmj.com/content/384/bmj.p2885.long). Discuss preferences and compare warm compress and massage in second stage; guidance rather than original trial data.
- **B3** — [Cochrane manual labor pain review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD009290_massage-reflexology-and-other-manual-methods-managing-pain-labour). Possible pain relief with massage and heat; uncertain effects on delivery outcomes, low or very low certainty.
- **B4** — [ACOG approaches to limit intervention](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2019/02/approaches-to-limit-intervention-during-labor-and-birth). Summarizes first-stage water immersion and position/relaxation evidence; outcome tradeoffs and clinical eligibility need separate review. Guidance, not independent primary data.
- **B5** — [2024 dates meta-analysis](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12884-023-06196-y). Signals on labor outcomes; includes randomized and nonrandomized trials, high-risk pregnancies excluded. Abadan University of Medical Sciences grant 1538; authors report no competing interests and no funder role.
- **B6** — [2020 dates meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32563673/). Possible shorter labor stages, but included evidence was methodologically weak. Do not treat reviews with overlapping trials as independent replication.
- **B7** — [Raspberry leaf integrative review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33563275/). Safety/efficacy question reviewed; companion NIH pregnancy supplement fact sheet reports null placebo comparison for several labor outcomes. No self-induction regimen.
- **C1** — [Zulu food practices ethnographic survey](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13002-021-00451-2). Documents practices, not health-effect causality. No project grant; author-generated research funds reported; authors declare no competing interest.
- **C2** — [Lao PDR traditional food restrictions study](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/mcn.13273). Describes postpartum food restrictions and dietary diversity; observational and culturally specific, not evidence for benefits.
- **C3** — [Review of Chinese postpartum doing the month](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24798890/). Separately assess support, rest, nutrition, hygiene and restrictions; do not generalize a culturally variable bundle as effective.
- **F1** — [Cochrane oral galactagogues review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7388198/). Includes 27 studies of natural oral products. Heterogeneity and uncertain adverse events limit inference; verify each ingredient and comparator. Full disclosure retrieval blocked.
- **F2** — [LactMed fenugreek](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK501779/). Long history does not establish effectiveness; limited safety data, reported adverse reactions, and supplement variability. Assess milk transfer and underlying causes first.
- **F3** — [Cochrane engorgement review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD006946_treatment-breast-engorgement-overfull-hard-painful-breasts-breastfeeding-women). Possible comfort benefit from cooling and some other approaches; low certainty. Do not extrapolate massage evidence to aggressive pressure or infection treatment.
- **I1** — [2025 Cochrane early skin-to-skin review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD003519_what-known-about-benefits-immediate-or-early-skin-skin-contact-mothers-and-babies). Evidence of some newborn physiological and breastfeeding benefits; appropriate positioning and airway safety required.
- **I2** — [WHO kangaroo mother care](https://www.who.int/tools/elena/interventions/kangaroo-care-infants). Population-specific evidence/guidance for preterm or low-birthweight infants; distinct from routine skin-to-skin.
- **I3** — [1986 infant carrying randomized trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3517799/). Historical trial lead for crying outcomes; replication and modern carrying safety need review.
- **I4** — [Cochrane infant massage review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD005038_massage-promoting-mental-and-physical-health-infants-under-age-six-months). Limited evidence of some outcomes; methodological weaknesses and applicability need examination.
- **I5** — [Infant colic systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26655941/). Signals for particular probiotic strains and fennel preparations; formulation and feeding population matter. Fennel not cleared for infant use; no dosing.

Prior packet references: TR-01 through TR-03 in [source-checks.json](source-checks.json); honey age boundary discussed in [README.md](README.md). Prior source IDs not counted as new leads.

## Integration instructions
1. Deduplicate against SOURCE_REGISTRY, existing stage-07 and child-health records and existing articles by claim, source DOI and underlying study; preserve independent contrary results. No evidence inflation from review overlap.
2. Prioritize S and C entries for source-specific appraisal; investigate L via neutral benefit/null/harm queries, ethnographies, registries, theses, multilingual archives, midwife/physio education, books and podcasts. Record exact media timestamp/page, creator and interests, and trace claims to primary data. For H use safety-only handling until clinical approval.
3. For every retained claim extract outcome, comparator, sample, effect and uncertainty, harms, timing, funding/COI, methodological bias and population applicability. Separate evidence of comfort from disease prevention or treatment. Publish no clinical guidance unless existing gates are met.
4. Group the approved reading into existing topic-first pages, not 120 new homepage cards. Cross-link using design/ARTICLE_CROSS_REFERENCES.md with actual destination IDs verified; retain original footnotes, avoid linking urgent warnings. Keep all leads and unreviewed outcomes in research archive only. Add actual app changes, tests, device acceptance and human review as separate checkpoints.
