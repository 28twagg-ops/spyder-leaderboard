## Findings and interpretation
- [USP nutrition doctoral thesis](https://teses.usp.br/teses/disponiveis/17/17139/tde-22052026-150941/pt-br.html), 350 randomized: no clear between-group diet/activity changes from counseling despite some within-group shifts. Higher later median cholesterol values in intervention group do not establish dietary causation. Preserve nulls, compare analysis plan and peer-reviewed publications; no weight or dietary restriction protocol.
- [USP photobiomodulation master's dissertation](https://teses.usp.br/teses/disponiveis/22/22133/tde-22112024-160927/en.html), 37 participants: two active groups showed within-group pain reductions at two hours, but placebo-adjusted effect and harms require extraction. No home laser recommendation.
- [China acupuncture trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37335994/), NCT04401384: active versus sham acupuncture MD −0.7 PUQE (95% CI −1.3 to −0.1), 352 participants at 13 mainland hospitals; clinical significance and safety remain separate. National Key R&D Program of China and Heilongjiang Province TouYan Innovation Team listed in published abstract. No independent status assumed.
- [South African isihlambezo thesis](https://researchspace.ukzn.ac.za/items/5dc0052f-8bc0-4cfb-b865-9991aa760a78): laboratory uterine activity and cell viability are not human pregnancy safety evidence. Retain solely as mechanistic/harm-research lead, no preparation, ingestion or induction protocols. Do not repeat author safety conclusion as fact.
- [Saudi breastfeeding thesis](https://etheses.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/34210/): mixed methods, 109 healthcare professionals plus maternal interviews; organizational/family barriers, not efficacy trial. CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 on repository page, link-only; no reuse permission assumed.

## Missing results / publication bias
[2023 obstetric trial publication study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37419451/): of 647 completed registered trials (2009–2018), 378 had journal publication and 269 did not at May 2021 search; author survey cited time, career/training changes and nonsignificant findings as barriers. Not proof of systematic suppression or favorable unpublished natural treatment findings. [Registry use audit](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27769265/) and [funding/publication associations](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32559607/) provide methods context.
