## Outcome-specific disagreement map (research only)
- **DRA and postpartum recovery:** 2026 review PMID 41995773, pooled IRD effect -0.06 cm (95% CI -1.19 to 1.08), only three sufficiently comparable trials; contrast favorable but very-low-certainty IRD estimates in PMID 40489856 and strength improvement without worsening IRD in PMID 37286390. Anatomy, function and symptoms are separate; do not promise closure or prohibit all curl-ups.
- **Calcium and preeclampsia:** updated 2025 Cochrane PMID 41330480, RR 0.83 (0.67–1.04), six RCTs, 15364 women, low certainty. 2026 commentary PMID 42374462 challenges the consistency of trial trustworthiness exclusions. Compare eligibility, baseline dietary intake, timing, harms, and clinical guidelines. Do not imply all calcium intake is ineffective or stop prescribed supplementation.
- **Vitamin D:** updated Cochrane PMID 39077939 versus favorable 2026 umbrella review PMID 41923026. Different eligibility, duplicate review cohorts and retracted primary studies need reconciliation. Nutritional adequacy, treating deficiency and additional supplementation for disease prevention are distinct questions.
- **Galactagogues:** Thai moringa RCT PMID 36440058, 88 randomized, day-three median milk volume 73.5 vs 50 mL, p=0.19. Authors' favorable conclusion is not established by the primary comparison; no general dosing/safety inference. Lactation support and infant intake assessment remain central.
- **Infant colic:** Australian RCT PMID 24690625, 49 minutes more crying/fussing daily at one month (95% CI 8–90) with the tested strain, especially formula-fed infants. Compare breastfed subgroups and other strain-specific RCTs, do not extrapolate to all probiotics. Trial charitable trust funding and separately disclosed author industry relationships documented in ledger.
- **Probiotics and gestational diabetes:** SPRING PMID 30659070, 38/207 vs 25/204 GDM; p=0.10. Preeclampsia 9.2% vs 4.9%, p=0.09; safety signal, not established harm. Compare prevention vs treatment studies and specific formulation.
- **Cervical pessary:** TOPS PMID 37490086, 544 randomized, no primary preterm-birth improvement and mortality safety signal RR 1.94 (1.13–3.32), stopped early. Compare different cervical lengths, concurrent progesterone and cerclage use. NICHD staff input and manuscript approval, author financial ties documented. Clinician-only; do not alter treatment independently.
- **Fetal movement:** AFFIRM PMID 30269876, package-level stillbirth OR 0.90 (0.75–1.07), erratum PMID 34338212 must be reconciled before numeric app use. Null package comparison never justifies ignoring reduced fetal movements; prompt assessment remains essential.
- **Acupuncture:** sham-controlled pain trial PMID 18947338 versus non-sham trial PMID 14995919; network review PMID 40509803 shows no clear advantage over sham. Nausea, pain, function, and safety outcomes are distinct. Do not interpret nonsignificant adverse-event comparison as proof of safety.
- **Medication comparisons:** EMPOWaR and GRoW metformin trials concern pregnancy in people with elevated BMI, not treatment of established diabetes. Swedish sibling-control acetaminophen study PMID 38592388 and Japanese bias analysis PMID 40898607 show why confounding by indication and familial factors require examination; no personal medication-change advice.
