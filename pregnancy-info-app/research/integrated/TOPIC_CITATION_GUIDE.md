## SKIN-001: Can creams prevent stretch marks?
**takeaway:** Moisturizers can help dryness. Reliable stretch-mark prevention is not established.

Selected sources: [S001](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD000066_topical-preparations-preventing-stretch-marks-pregnancy).

**action:** Moisturize for comfort, not as a required prevention routine.

Selected sources: [S001](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD000066_topical-preparations-preventing-stretch-marks-pregnancy).

**safety:** During pregnancy, check ingredients, especially retinoids. Stop irritating products. Prevention studies give little information about uncommon adverse effects.

Selected sources: [S204](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-secrets/routine/pregnancy-skin-care).

## SKIN-002: Is collagen worth it?
**takeaway:** Adult skin studies disagree. Pregnancy stretch-mark prevention is unproven.

Selected sources: [S011](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0002934325002839), [S012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43047141/).

**action:** Keep collagen off the required checklist.

Selected sources: [S011](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0002934325002839).

**safety:** Before conception, adult cosmetic findings apply only to similar populations. During pregnancy and lactation, product-specific safety and benefit for this indication remain unestablished.

Selected sources: [N323](https://www.medlineplus.gov/druginfo/natural/1606.html), [N324](https://doi.org/10.1071/HC15947).

## SKIN-003: Can stretch marks fade?
**takeaway:** Treatments may improve existing marks, but complete removal is not guaranteed.

Selected sources: [S106](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/skin-conditions-during-pregnancy), [S018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30618025/).

**action:** Consider a dermatologist after recovery if the marks bother you.

Selected sources: [S106](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/skin-conditions-during-pregnancy), [S018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30618025/).

**safety:** Defer elective procedures during pregnancy. Later assessment must account for healing, site, skin type and lactation. RF microneedling has a device-specific FDA harm warning.

## BREAST-001: What changes breast shape?
**takeaway:** Pregnancy changes breast tissue. Research does not establish a cosmetic reason to avoid breastfeeding.

Selected sources: [S044](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19083576/).

**action:** Choose comfortable support and the feeding approach you prefer.

Selected sources: [S044](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19083576/).

**safety:** Support may be chosen across stages for comfort. A cosmetic procedure during recovery or lactation needs specialist assessment. Do not frame formula, breastfeeding or combined feeding as a cosmetic intervention.
