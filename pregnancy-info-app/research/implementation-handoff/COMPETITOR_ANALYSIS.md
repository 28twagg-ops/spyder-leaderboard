# Comparator research

Review of public publisher pages and app-store descriptions, not an installed-app usability or screenshot audit. Claims of expert review and efficacy are vendor descriptions unless independently supported. Do not copy protected assets, branding, content or layouts.

| Product/resource | Observed public feature | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|
| [Pregnancy+](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pregnancy-tracker-app/id505864483) | interactive 3D fetal models, stage guides, milestone timeline | Borrow navigable, stage-specific anatomical visualizations; avoid making fetal size or private photos the core of a maternal preparation app. |
| [BabyCenter](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/babycenter-pregnancy-tracker/id386022579) | growth tracking, staged content, community, expert review claimed | Use phased continuity and concise updates; do not import community anecdotes into clinical evidence. |
| [Ovia](https://www.oviahealth.com/pregnancy-app/) | milestones, symptom and medication/food lookups | Borrow searchable lookups and context tags; avoid unnecessary data collection and never present app as prescribing authority. |
| [Ovia app listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ovia-cycle-pregnancy-tracker/id570244389) | weekly videos, personalized recovery pathways, partner sharing | Delivery-specific content and optional sharing; private by default. |
| [Expecting and Empowered](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/expecting-and-empowered/id1594936110) | structured prenatal/postpartum workouts, delivery pathways | Borrow manageable, phase-appropriate demonstration pattern; marketing efficacy claims not independently verified. |
| [NHS Best Start in Life](https://www.nhs.uk/best-start-in-life/pregnancy/week-by-week-guide-to-pregnancy/) | weekly body/baby information and practical next steps | Clear stage-based education with visible care links; jurisdiction tag. |
| [Tommy's PregnancyHub](https://www.tommys.org/about-us/what-we-do/we-provide-information-make-pregnancy-safer) | midwife-led information with advisory oversight | Borrow transparent review and safety escalation. |
| [Evidence Based Birth](https://evidencebasedbirth.com/signature-article-directory/) | topic evidence articles, podcast and handouts | Borrow layered evidence narratives and optional media; verify original studies independently. |
| [CDC Hear Her](https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/) | urgent warning-sign education and stories | Borrow visible warning signs and partner escalation, not dramatized appearance messaging. |

Market-level research: [2024 analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39587534/) reviewed 36 apps and raised credibility/commercialization concerns; [pregnancy app safety scoping review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37675058/) emphasizes evidence, transparency, privacy and tailoring. This does not establish comparative safety or rank individual apps.

The proposed differentiator is a long preconception-to-postpartum maternal-function timeline, quick visual cards backed by inspectable evidence, research disagreement with transparent methods, and privacy-preserving minimal daily planning. This is a product hypothesis, not a verified market gap. Test before claiming novelty.
