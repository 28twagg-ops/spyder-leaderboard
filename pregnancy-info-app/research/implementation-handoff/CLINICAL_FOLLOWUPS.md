# Clinical follow-up evidence map

These are targeted source checks on open backlog items. Existing registry entries may overlap; do not add the counts to 223 or treat them as independent studies. Full articles are inspected where specified; indexed sources and blocked pages remain limited. No claim of completing a systematic review or medical approval.

## CL01 · SKIN-002 — 2026 updated adult collagen meta-analysis
35 trials, 2534 healthy adults. Initial elasticity SMD approximately 0.00 (95% CI −0.99–0.98); after excluding two high-leverage studies 0.62 (0.15–1.10). Sensitivity results for wrinkles −0.06 (−0.18–0.06). Substantial heterogeneity. No pregnancy striae endpoint; no prenatal safety conclusion.

Access: full article sections read. [Original source](https://doi.org/10.1111/jocd.71041).

## CL02 · SKIN-002 — 2026 technical reanalysis of collagen reviews
Raises extraction and statistical problems including SE/SD mix-ups and no detected dose response. Does not support recommendation for aging. Overlapping trials, not independent vote; primary extraction still needed.

Access: indexed article sections. [Original source](https://doi.org/10.1111/jocd.71208).

## CL03 · PELV-001 — 2024 pressure biofeedback adjunct RCT
452 analyzed; new postpartum stress incontinence within 12 weeks, five hospitals, China; supervised training with versus without device for 3 months. Cure 45/223 vs 20/229; improvement 132/223 vs 102/229. Severe tears, DRA, chronic pain and other groups excluded. NCT05115864. Not preventive or a universal device prescription.

Access: full article methods and abstract. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11539013/).

## CL04 · PELV-001 — 2026 PEFLOW prevention RCT
764 randomized, singleton primiparous, China; training from week 28; 6-week postpartum SUI 32/367 vs 50/360; authors report risk difference favoring intervention 5.17 percentage points (95% CI 0.36–10.03). ChiCTR2000029618; no COI reported. Excluded severe complications and existing SUI/prolapse. Funding not extracted.

Access: abstract and disclosures. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41984478/).

## CL05 · FIT-002 — 2025 prenatal resistance training review
50 studies, 47,619 participants; 90% multicomponent programs, mostly low-to-moderate resistance intensity. Gestational hypertension OR 0.42 (0.27–0.66); GDM OR 0.62 (0.48–0.79). Cannot isolate resistance training effect; dosage/progression poorly reported. No competing interests declared.

Access: abstract and disclosures. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40610191/).

## CL06 · BIRTH-001 — BUMPES second-stage position RCT
3093 analyzed, first birth, term singleton, low-dose epidural. Upright spontaneous vaginal births 548/1556 vs lying-down 632/1537; adjusted RR 0.86 (0.78–0.94). Not a universal position mandate or cosmetic outcome.

Access: article and summary. [Original source](https://www.bmj.com/content/359/bmj.j4471).

## CL07 · BIRTH-001 — 2018 immediate versus delayed pushing RCT
2404 analyzed with neuraxial analgesia; spontaneous delivery 1031/1200 vs 1041/1204; RR 0.99 (0.96–1.03). Do not conflate with BUMPES or make universal labor instructions.

Access: article summary. [Original source](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2706136).

## CL08 · BIRTH-001 — 2024 ACOG labor management guidance
Consider pushing evidence in context of labor, patient preferences, clinician judgment and neuraxial analgesia. Not a self-directed birth plan protocol.

Access: guidance excerpt. [Original source](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/clinical-practice-guideline/articles/2024/01/first-and-second-stage-labor-management).

## CL09 · FACE-001 — 2007 pregnancy sunscreen study
200 enrolled, 185 completed, 5 new melasma cases (2.7%). Compared against previously observed 53%, not concurrent randomized controls. Do not use historical rates as treatment effect. Formulation-specific; funding not extracted.

Access: abstract. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17567299/).

## CL10 · BREAST-001 — 2008 breastfeeding and breast aesthetics study
Retrospective aesthetic surgery consultation sample; breastfeeding not independent risk factor. Selection/confounding limits causality. No robust prospective prevention trial established.

Access: abstract. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19083576/).

## CL11 · HAIR-001 — 2023 postpartum hair-loss questionnaire
Cross-sectional self-report, not a longitudinal measurement of recovery. Do not use it to infer feeding causes hair loss or counsel against breastfeeding.

Access: indexed methods. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10846762/).

## CL12 · HAIR-001 — 2024 postpartum shedding and underlying disorders
200 patients already presenting with shedding at dermatology clinic; selection prevents population prevalence inference. Persistent/atypical loss needs evaluation.

Access: indexed methods. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11107900/).

## CL13 · SCAR-001 — 2021 Cochrane silicone sheeting
Evidence for hypertrophic scar treatment limited; distinguish closed/healed scars, prevention, pain and surgical scars. Do not recommend on open wounds.

Access: abstract/indexed. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34564840/).

## CL14 · SCAR-001 — Cesarean silicone sheet vs gel randomized trial
Horizontal cesarean population and active comparator. Cannot establish benefit versus no treatment without suitable comparison. Numerical outcomes and funding remain unextracted.

Access: indexed abstract; full text blocked. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30095630/).

## CL15 · VEIN-001 — 2015 Cochrane varicose veins and oedema
Seven small trials, 326 women. Review concluded insufficient reliable evidence; distinguish compression for symptom comfort versus prevention of spider veins. Urgent clot warning signs separate.

Access: indexed full article. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7050615/).

## CL16 · MSK-001 — 2008 acupuncture vs sham pelvic girdle pain RCT
Direct sham comparison identified; full result extraction pending. Compare with exercise/standard-care trial and avoid claiming mechanism or efficacy until extracted.

Access: indexed abstract; source page blocked. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18947338/).

## CL17 · WELL-001 — 2019 prenatal insomnia CBT-I RCT
194 randomized, 179 analyzed; median remission time 31 vs 48 days, 63.8% vs 51.9% remission at end of treatment. Treatment in symptomatic patients, not universal prevention; no self-directed sleep restriction recommendation.

Access: indexed full article. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6485299/).

## CL18 · WELL-001 — 2020 digital CBT-I RCT
208 randomized with insomnia symptoms; reported remission 44.0% (30) vs 22.3% (21). Denominators not separately extracted; do not reconstruct rounded percentages.

Access: indexed article. [Original source](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamapsychiatry/fullarticle/2758827).

## CL19 · DENT-001 — 2024 periodontal care review
Lower-quality studies favorable for birth outcomes; higher-quality studies not significant. Needed dental care remains important.

Access: indexed abstract. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39721768/).

## CL20 · DENT-001 — 2025 dental treatment review
Newer review suggests possible preterm birth benefit; trial overlap, intervention mix, certainty and disease severity not extracted. Do not update clinical recommendation based on abstract alone.

Access: indexed abstract; full text blocked. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41421601/).

## CL21 · REPRO-001 — ACOG prepregnancy counseling
Medication review, health history, immunization and appropriate genetic counseling; recheck schedules near use. Not a directive to stop medications.

Access: guideline excerpt. [Original source](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2019/01/prepregnancy-counseling).

## CL22 · REPRO-001 — 2026 paternal nutrition evidence gap map
75 studies: 60 animal and 15 human observational; not proof of specific paternal supplement benefit. Partner health habits and support, not speculative dosing.

Access: indexed abstract. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42417057/).

## CL23 · EMERG-001 — FDA microneedling devices and safety
Indication-specific authorization, pigment changes and other harms; no pregnancy clearance. Do not provide at-home procedure protocols.

Access: public guidance. [Original source](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/aesthetic-cosmetic-devices/microneedling-devices).

## CL24 · EMERG-001 — 2026 RF microneedling MAUDE analysis
86 reports, 145 reported adverse events for 2020–2025; reports not unique patient incidence; exposure denominator unavailable. Cannot infer rate or compare devices.

Access: indexed abstract. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42430734/).

### Priority conclusions for integration
Collagen remains unproven for pregnancy striae. The 2025 funding-stratified review (S011), 2026 favorable sensitivity analysis (CL01) and 2026 technical criticism (CL02) should be displayed together, with overlapping study sets and no vote-count percentages. Distinguish adult hydration/elasticity from structural outcomes and pregnancy prevention. CL01's primary and outlier-excluded results differ; do not silently replace one with the other.

PEFLOW (CL04) addresses prevention in a selected prenatal group, whereas biofeedback (CL03) addresses established postpartum stress leakage. Do not combine outcomes. In birth decisions, BUMPES compares position and Cahill compares pushing timing; stage, epidural and patient preference are crucial. No cosmetic indication.

Remaining gaps include complete primary trial funding/registrations, adverse event denominators, breast prospective cohorts, scar and pigmentation harms, isolated ingredients, device indications, nutrition-specific subgroups and systematic screening.
