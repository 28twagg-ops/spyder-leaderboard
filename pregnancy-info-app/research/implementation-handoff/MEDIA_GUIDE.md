# Media integration

17 candidate links to public clinical, health agency or education publisher resources. These are not downloaded assets, and playback, duration, captions, frame-level clinical suitability and licenses have not been verified. A collection is not a single video. For every promoted item, confirm the actual media, its publisher, date, duration, transcript/captions, relevant stage, safety notes, locale and permission to link/embed. If unavailable, retain an accessible text/diagram fallback. Never bypass access or scrape copyrighted transcripts.

Default presentation: user-initiated playback; preview title, short purpose, stage and safety note; external link until embed privacy and permissions reviewed; no autoplay or third-party trackers before consent. No video is necessary to understand urgent warnings. Do not reuse NHS graphics as app assets without license verification. Prefer official medical/health-agency channels for instruction, and separate commercial or lived-experience resources from scientific evidence.

For topics lacking a suitable audited video (stretch marks, collagen, breast appearance, scars beyond healing, emerging devices), prioritize original non-idealized diagrams or short educational animations reviewed by an appropriate expert. Do not invent a video URL or claim that a procedure clip is safe to follow.

Use AHRQ [PEMAT-A/V](https://www.ahrq.gov/health-literacy/patient-education/pemat-av.html) to score understandability and actionability separately, then conduct medical and accessibility review. Candidate catalog includes topic IDs for lookup.
