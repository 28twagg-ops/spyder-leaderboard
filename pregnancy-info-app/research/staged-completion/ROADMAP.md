# Approval-gated research completion stages
Only run one authorized stage at a time. Stop and report results before starting next. No recurring monitoring. Regular ChatGPT first; Work preferred only for large file operations, running validation on full checkout, exports and final document/asset packaging.
1. Scientific integrity, funding standards and safety reconciliation — initial scoped audit complete; ongoing individual disclosures and clinical approval remain.
2. Breastfeeding/formula, diapers, elimination communication, parenting alternatives — scoped packet complete; [Stage 2](stage-02/README.md). Remaining primary-source verification recorded. Stage 3 requires user approval.
3. Maternal health, rehabilitation, appearance preservation.
4. Fetal and childhood cognitive, motor and emotional development.
5. Nutrition, supplements and ingredients, trial reconciliation.
6. Environmental exposures and long-term health.
7. Natural, emerging, controversial interventions.
8. Existing textbook diagrams, figures, videos and illustrations; rights/accuracy checks and asset manifest. Use original source materials rather than AI where suitable; source candidates can be collected throughout stages.
9. Practical routines, educational articles, parallel timelines.
10. Work preferred: consolidated research manifest, canonical/staged crosswalk, reproducible export regeneration and full QA.
11. Work preferred: owner PDF/Word/Excel/ZIP deliverables and final Claude handoff.
For each stage: inspect main; extract actual studies, quantify effects and harms, audit funding/COI, preserve holds, add source-linked records and citations, note full-text vs abstract access, update transfer, verify commit, report scope and limitations. Clinical review and real app tests remain separate. Do not start a later stage without approval.
