## Main findings
Prioritize known lead, unsafe water, smoke, alcohol, mercury exposure, workplace hazards and heat; ordinary precautions for emerging risks, no consumer fear campaign. Lead nonlinear pooled child IQ association is not individual prediction; no safe blood level. Lower-mercury fish rather than eliminating seafood. 
PFOS: 2020 review 29 studies funded via 3M-Ramboll contract; salary/employment and sponsor role disclosed, authors retained manuscript control. Overall and early observed associations negative, modeled intercept near zero; causal support judged weak. 2025 EPA 53-study analysis found negative association overall and early sample group, timing not explanatory. Coauthor Dzierlenga appears in both with publication-specific affiliations. Different units (linear concentration vs natural log), overlap, timing and no sponsor-specific pooling. PFNA and PFUnDA are separate chemicals. ATSDR/NASEM guidance differs; no automatic PFAS testing/feeding changes. 2026 EPA proposals not final status verified.
Funding examples: 2016 atrazine animal review 39-study subset 11 industry/24 nonindustry/4 unknown, effect-size sponsorship association inconclusive, no identified methodological risk difference; historical 2006 BPA narrative review 11/11 industry studies no significant effect, 109 significant low-dose animal studies overall; no independent denominator. Neither represents modern human bias rate. FDA/EFSA BPA scientific positions differ; CLARITY animal data separate. 
Phthalates: pooled 16 cohorts and ECHO share at least five cohort labels; no independent replication claim. Publicly funded studies have individual external ties (see P6-21 and P6-22). Fresh food short intervention decreased BPA/DEHP markers; another NIEHS-supported randomized food replacement unexpectedly increased DEHP due ingredient contamination; not infant clinical outcomes. HERMOSA adolescent biomarker evidence indirect. Organic food can reduce some pesticide metabolites; do not displace produce or imply better development.
Microplastic tissue/bottle detection, rapid hazard review and 2026 direct human review: no quantified causal infant risk, moderate/high measurement/confounding risk; FDA food assessment differs in scope. Never weaken formula sterilization, temperature or ratios. Human review date discrepancy flagged. 
Air: UGAAR publicly funded plus Coway discounted devices; cognition CI includes zero, 29% lower particles, same cohort growth follow-up. HAPIN NIH/Gates birth weight and stunting null, biomass-to-LPG setting not US kitchen comparison. HEPA for particle reduction, not all gases or proven IQ benefit. Heat observational associations, avoid personal risk thresholds.
Both parents: preconception review 63 studies, search through 2021, no pooled result; paternal scoping review and heat semen surrogate data distinct from offspring outcomes. Workplace source controls and take-home precautions. Water, mold, cleaning, carbon monoxide and radon guidance source-first.
