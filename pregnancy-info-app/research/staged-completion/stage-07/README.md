Scoped research checkpoint: 68 mixed source records (reviews, trials, guidance and prior aliases; not independent study count), 41 numerical records, 16 comparisons, 4 transparency examples, 9 practical draft actions, 12 visual links, 21 holds and 15 synthetic safety scenarios.
## Key findings
- Ginger: 2024 Gates-supported review (funder chose topic, no other role declared), seven reviews all low/critically low AMSTAR-2; none reported component funding. October 2026 network review no external funding, academic publication support, no COI declared; VAS favorable low confidence, PUQE inconsistent; safety incomplete. Mild nausea only; severe vomiting escalation.
- Dates: Abadan University grant 1538, no declared COI; 4/15 randomized trials low risk, 33 nonrandomized reports; timing and outcomes separate. Promising food, not home induction. Raspberry leaf evidence insufficient (funding N/A, author PhD interest); castor oil not supported for self-induction. Moxibustion moderate certainty for non-cephalic birth reduction but not cesarean, sparse harms; 2016 unfunded trial null.
- Labor support, massage, relaxation and water immersion are optional comfort approaches; continuous support review low certainty for several favorable birth outcomes. Acupuncture sham comparisons may help satisfaction but IVF live birth not clearly improved in public-funded sham trial with disclosed business interests or pooled evidence. No routine induction or analgesia avoidance.
- Lactation: oral galactagogue review cannot pool natural products (I2 99% for milk volume). University-funded moringa RCT primary result p=.19 despite favorable 47% descriptive language, no COI declared. No herbal substitute for feeding assessment; domperidone FDA cardiac/withdrawal warning. Fenugreek evidence inconsistent.
- Red/infrared therapy: 60-participant perineal sham RCT no superiority, no specific grant/no COI, CAPES publication support. Nipple evidence low certainty, different endpoint; one indexed interval malformed, quarantined. Cosmetic RF and PRP claims preliminary, FDA serious RF microneedling harms preserved; new postpartum dyspareunia trial provisional, disclosures unknown. No procedural advice.
- Infant probiotics: NHMRC-funded BioGaia in-kind trial reports +49 min/day crying/fussing at one month; parent-trial unfavorable result retained; 2018 IPD includes that trial and reports -25.4 min/day change at day21, stronger breastfed result and many author ties. Different time and subgroup; no independent-only estimate. Abstract strain typo flagged. Preterm NEC review potential benefit but smallest subgroup uncertain and FDA invasive disease warning; NICU decisions only.
- Infant massage and manual therapies: no spinal manipulation or mobilization in infants per 2024 position statement; low-risk ordinary touch differs from procedures. Placenta capsules no demonstrated iron benefit and CDC GBS case; avoid. Vaginal seeding randomized results mixed, public NZ pilot null, neonatal microbial surrogate and short ASQ measures not durable benefit; ACOG/AAP research-only due infection.
- Kangaroo care: low-cost human contact in clinical neonatal care supported by multinational trial with philanthropic WHO grant; not experimental DIY care. Updated 2026 Cochrane abstract funding none, older web-summary numbers not reused. Yoga/mind-body activity can support mood but not replace care.
