# Independence and commercial influence standard — stage 1
User preference: question commercial and conventional claims, alternatives and historical practices equally; prioritize independently replicated evidence and disclose financial interests prominently.
For every claim define PICO, seek unfavorable/null evidence and direct clinical outcomes. Track direct funding, ultimate funding entity where publicly verifiable, intermediaries, grants, gifts and product donations, author employment/consulting/patents/equity, sponsor control of design/analysis/data access/writing/publication, registration and protocol deviations, adverse event reporting, and independent replication. Record exact locator and distinguish missing from declared none. Do not infer concealed ties without evidence. Audit guideline panel disclosures and systematic review underlying trials separately. Funding groups: declared noncommercial with verified disclosures; mixed/in-kind; commercially interested; unclear/unreported; not applicable. Verify ultimate source where possible; never label unknown independent.
Apply RoB2/ROBINS-I or appropriate review appraisal, outcome-specific GRADE and separate funding/COI overlay. Prefer robust independent evidence when similarly direct and precise. Sponsor-controlled analysis, missing protocol, selective reporting, or unexplained discrepancies warrant documented downgrade or quarantine. Commercial-only marketing efficacy claims stay provisional until independent confirmation or exceptionally strong transparent evidence and reviewer approval. Do not multiply effect sizes or inverse-variance weights by arbitrary penalties. Where eligible data allow, report all-eligible, independent-only, low-risk-only, and industry/mixed subgroup sensitivity estimates; avoid pooling incompatible outcomes and double counting. If independent results differ, expose direction, precision, population and methodological differences. No evidence of independence is not proof of misconduct. No manufacturer funding does not guarantee quality. Apply same disclosure demands to cloth diaper brands, lactation advocacy, supplement vendors, professional groups and alternative treatments.
Regulatory safety standards are a separate question from comparative superiority. Never discourage adequate infant nutrition, necessary medications or established safety measures based solely on funding suspicion. No home formula or forced toilet training. Preserve medical safety and qualified review gates.
