# Compare findings by funding and methodological quality
User approved 2026-11-26. On material disputed claims provide a simple default synthesis prioritizing direct, high-quality, independently replicated evidence. Also expose All evidence, no identified relevant commercial ties after disclosure verification, industry direct funding, industry in-kind or relevant author ties/mixed, unknown disclosure; separate optional low-risk-of-bias view. Academic affiliation alone never proves independence. No group labeled "completely unbiased." Funding and methodological quality are orthogonal dimensions. Never hide evidence unfavorable to sponsor; flag reported null or adverse outcomes relevant to the sponsor's product as valuable counterevidence, not automatic proof of integrity. Track outcome and comparator, not a paper-level positive/negative label. Show exact study IDs, cohort grouping, relevant ties and ultimate entity, sponsor role, author affiliations at publication, protocol and prespecified outcome, directness, effect and interval, limitations. Unknowns remain unknown. For each group summarize agreement, disagreement, why results might differ; explain if insufficient independently verified evidence. Display actual comparable outcomes where available; avoid vote counts as a measure of certainty. No subgroup causal conclusion from different significance; require formal interaction test or clearly descriptive contrast. No arbitrary multipliers or automatically excluding industry data. Use independently verified high-quality evidence first when comparable, but preserve the total evidence, harms and established preventive care. If the only adequate evidence is sponsored, explain dependence and residual uncertainty; do not invent a different conclusion. Reviews and primary trial financing separate; separate noncommercial foundations from company-origin foundations and trade groups, verify direct or indirect source. Do not filter out safety signals, including sponsored negative results. Show grade 7–8 quick explanation and optional deeper source comparisons. Named-company percentage only for a defined deduplicated universe with audited ties. Claude implements presentation; all content research-only until qualified review.
