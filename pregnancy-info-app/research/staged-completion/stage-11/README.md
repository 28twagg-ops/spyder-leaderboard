## Design and scope
iPhone first, Home Screen launch, responsive browser/other devices; Claude owns architecture and implementation. The app covers preparation for pregnancy, pregnancy, postpartum recovery, and fetal/infant/child health through age two with secondary coverage through five. Parallel maternal and child clocks after birth, corrected age for appropriate development only, not acute illness. Plain-language quick view targeted at grade 7–8, expanded details and original evidence; no health score, appearance score, milestone race or required three-year schedule. Existing licensed artwork preferred, no unapproved asset use. Independent-first evidence comparison must display uncertainty, sponsor ties, unknowns, harms, and sponsor-unfavorable findings. Funding is not a synonym for methodological bias.
