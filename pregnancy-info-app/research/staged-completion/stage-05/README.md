Calcium 2025 Cochrane and Oct 2026 IPD no clear overall benefit; important correction to DR003: nonlinear gestational timing interaction and modeled 10-week result, not a recommendation. Commentary no specific grant but prior review authorship conflicts. India/Tanzania trials compare doses not placebo; WHO low-intake guidance remains referenced; no care changes. Probiotic risk signal and broader null review differ in eligibility; preserve SPRING in-kind negative signal. Iron reduces anemia; alternate-day trial not clearly superior.
