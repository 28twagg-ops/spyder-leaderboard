## Key conclusions and funding distinctions
- Stretch marks: topical review S001 uncertain. Manufacturer ET Browne supplied both lotions in negative cocoa butter study S004. Almond oil massage study cannot separate massage from oil. RP004 coconut oil trial reports 25% vs 45% incidence; full methods, funding, attrition and replication pending. Do not promote a 3-year prevention schedule or definitive product claims.
- Collagen: S011 2025 review reports disappearance of clear effects in non-pharma-funded/high-quality subgroups. CL02 2026 reanalysis Table 1 documents errors (SE vs SD, incompatible units, omissions); Table 2/figure 1 examines dose; hydration 4/7 industry-funded significant vs 0/2 nonsponsored, elasticity all significant from sponsored trials vs 0/3 nonsponsored. Counts descriptive only. S012 2026 review finds some instrumental effects. Overlap and primary re-extraction pending; no direct pregnancy prevention or breast appearance benefit established.
- Breast: CGATE pregnancy volume changes; S044 selected surgery cohort does not identify breastfeeding as independent ptosis predictor. No causal reassurance or feeding pressure; support garments for comfort not proven structural prevention.
- DRA: Gluppe RCT strength and thickness gains without IRD worsening; Beamish rest IRD -.52 cm (5 trials), Lyons TrA-only -.06 cm (3 trials), other 2026 review uses mm and broader comparisons. Distinct interventions, cointerventions, outcomes, sites, timing and common trials. No pooling these reviews, no universal gap target or anatomical cure. New 2025 pregnancy TrA/RA trial does not support blanket prohibition.
- PFPT after OASIS: PFDI-20 median change and IQR preserved (not CI). NIH/Harvard support and no COI reported. AfterBabyBodyStudy region-funded, no COI reported; observational, stress UI persists. Beamish author funding documented, trial funding separate. Pelvic overactivity requires individualized assessment.
- Exercise: 48 trained athletes in acute hemodynamic study, one symptomatic vena cava episode; Norwegian women's public health association reported in indexed funding text. No blanket lifting or supine safety claim. Canadian 120 min/wk goal gradual and symptom-based; US guidance and clinical exceptions remain controlling.
- Perineal massage, pelvic girdle pain, binders and scars: distinguish antenatal/intrapartum/postpartum, symptom support vs anatomical correction, silicone on appropriate healed wounds, mixed funding (Elden insurer plus foundations). Sham comparison tempers acupuncture claims. FDA RF microneedling adverse event warning preserved. Retinoid hold preserved.
- Maternal urgent warning signs: CDC pregnancy through one year postpartum. No exercise or cosmetic guidance overrides escalation.
