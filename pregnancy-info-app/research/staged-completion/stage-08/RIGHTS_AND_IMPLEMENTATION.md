# Rights and implementation
Rights clearance and clinical/accessibility approval are separate gates. All production flags false. Prefer CC BY, CC0, explicit agency reuse and original source artwork. Preserve exact source, license version, credit, modification notice, no implied endorsement. CC BY-SA derivative obligations and NC-SA commercial limitations matter. Public access, free viewing, open article abstracts and YouTube do not imply redistribution. Separate third-party photos and logos. Respect child likeness/privacy, no unrelated sensitive association.
License evidence: legacy individual Commons file pages; OpenStax 2e https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/preface ; CDC https://www.cdc.gov/Other/imagereuse.html and https://wwwn.cdc.gov/PHIL/FAQ.aspx (credit and no endorsement); NICHD https://safetosleep.nichd.nih.gov/resources/social-digital (specific infographics and embed code, separate logo permission); WHO https://www.who.int/about/policies/publishing/copyright ; NHS https://www.nhs.uk/our-policies/terms-and-conditions/ plus exclusions (individual trusts separate); Harvard https://developingchild.harvard.edu/terms-and-conditions/ and contact; Servier https://smart.servier.com/how-to-cite-servier-medical-art/ ; Bioicons https://github.com/duerrsimon/bioicons . 
Prefer approved local files, not hotlinks. Link-only opens original page on user action; no unlicensed download or automatic iframe/trackers, no health information in URL, no automatic live syndication. Before use: inspect actual image/watch full video, verify individual credits/license, clinical labels, stage, safe positioning, captions and accessible static alternative. Large text, zoom, reduced motion, pause, no color alone. Never hide safety in a video. Research charts preserve units, baseline, interval, denominator, cohort, funding and caveats; no chart from held estimates. Use source figures in research view when eligible and simple data-based charts for quick view.
SVGs preserve pinned originals; don't inline unknown markup; conservative text scan is not full security audit. Clinical review and actual render pending. Do not infer species, stage or anatomy from icon filename. No generated medical illustration unless needed and reviewed.
