# Content and practical routine contract
Five priority labels: urgent (immediate escalation), essential (established prevention/safety or medical care), recommended (supported ordinary practices), optional (preference/comfort, uncertain extra benefit), research_only (not a prescribed action). Priority is editorial navigation, not GRADE. Research-only records cannot generate routine tasks, reminders, product suggestions or dosages; urgent records always override filters. Actions attached to research-only articles are questions/education, not interventions. Phase/article links identify relevant reading, not a prescription or due date. All records draft; no publication until qualified review.
Quick view target grade 7–8 with safety visible before expandable detail. Research view shows actual original sources, outcome definitions, risk and uncertainty, funding and conflicts, unknowns, overlapping cohorts, independent and commercial evidence comparison according to FUNDING_AND_READABILITY_STANDARD and COMPARE_EVIDENCE_STANDARD. Do not infer absence of a company tie from missing fields. No percentages except defined audited denominators. No sponsor-based suppression of harms. Use Stage 8 rights and clinical gates; no automatic embedding.
Preconception phases flexible and may be skipped; no universal three-year prescription. Pregnancy uses clinician-established gestational age (not fertilization date). Birth is shared anchor; postpartum and infant tracks independent. Chronological age for acute illness; corrected age only with appropriate developmental context, never fever thresholds. No diagnostic ranges, appearance grades, IQ scores, development rankings, weight targets or compulsive tracking. Prioritize safety, affordable food, ordinary care and family autonomy. No forced breastfeeding, delivery mode, toilet training, exercise, sex or supplements. No personalized doses or schedules. No user health data in research repository.
Release gates: qualified maternal/pediatric and emergency content review, source/version/date check, exact funding and claim validation, retraction and hold resolution, asset rights and accessibility review, tested emergency behavior offline and online, no unsafe recommendation from filter or clock, user testing of readability, actual device QA. Existing data remain authoritative only after reconciliation, not because a newer draft exists.
Known gaps: more detailed symptom-specific articles, complex pregnancy and neonatal pathways, direct stage-specific evidence for all actions, quantitative food portions and product-specific prices, postpartum chronic-condition follow-up and comprehensive age 3–5 coverage. Do not generate unsupported clinical care instructions to fill gaps. Stage 10 integrates and validates; clinical review separate.

Home screen: feature only two to four relevant starting points per phase plus persistent safety. Display conditional content (feeding type, cesarean care, prematurity, individual needs) only as relevant education, not automated medical assignment. Do not make a completed action or skipped action affect a health score. Maternal warnings through year one; emergency care remains reachable thereafter. Pediatric fever content retains its age scope; no generic reassurance in older phases.
