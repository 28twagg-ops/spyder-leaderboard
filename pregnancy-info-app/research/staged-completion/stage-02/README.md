## Feeding
AHRQ 2025 finds several favorable associations but no clear cognitive association after accounting for evidence quality. PROBIT randomized support, not exclusive milk versus formula, and the age-16 verbal effect is modest. Public funders and NeuroTrax author employment disclosed. Sibling comparisons attenuate broad long-term associations but remain observational. Preserve original PED-PROBIT, PED-DONOR and infant allergy records. Human milk has dynamic immune/bioactive constituents; regulated formula is a necessary safe nutritional option when prepared appropriately. No homemade or diluted alternatives.
Formula claims audit: 125-trial methods review shows widespread industry ties and bias; 2023 survey has no external funding but full author disclosures remain open. Nestlé HMO trial with direct grant/in-kind support and several employed authors has promising secondary effects; no independent confirmation established by this pass. DHA/ARA evidence and hydrolyzed allergy prevention do not justify claims of universal superior development. Do not confuse allergy treatment with prevention.
## Diapers
Historical Zurich cohorts (320 vs 309) show median training onset 13 months later, bowel control 16 months later, no reported difference in bladder control. Cross-cultural Vietnamese cohort illustrates assisted training, not independent continence. 2023 web survey supports hypothesis of earlier training with diaper-free/cloth use but confounding and recall bias. 2025 Antwerp crossover observes reduced cues with disposable diapers in 23 complete cases; methods and results likely same cohort. BABITT randomized trial (271 allocated, 259 complete) found no clear early GI benefit, not a test of independent toilet training age; later follow-up is not verified. Funding publicly disclosed; no specific diaper-company financing established for those reports. Commercial involvement flagged in 2021 diaper review remains unverified and must not be asserted as fact. Cochrane rash review old, weak evidence; hygiene guidance applies to both types.
## Alternatives
Responsive carrying, massage, behavioral settling and assisted toileting considered with limitations; ordinary interaction need not be commercialized. No unsafe sleep, forced toileting, restrictive feeding or unreviewed medical instructions.
