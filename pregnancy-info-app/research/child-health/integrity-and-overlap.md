# Publication integrity and overlap

## Paternal exercise and fertility
The [existing audit](../deep-research/TARGETED_GAP_AUDIT.json) retains DR023–025 as provenance only. The retractions were independently checked in this pass: [DR023 notice](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38030527/), [DR024 notice](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37277302/), [DR025 notice](https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000004451). DR021 includes these as references 9, 8 and 11, respectively. DR022 includes DR021 at reference 34. Exclude the three trials from clinical conclusions and block dependency-derived effects and rankings. No new calculation was performed. [DR021, Results and references](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9214906/) · [DR022, Table 1](https://jogh.org/2026/jogh-16-04282)

**Additional outcome-specific hold.** Lo Giudice et al. explicitly pooled studies 8, 9 and 10 for pregnancy and live birth (1,297 participants). Reference 10 is the retracted DR023. DR022 also includes Lo Giudice at reference 38. Block both pooled outcomes and their downstream use. This edge does not by itself invalidate semen estimates based on a different reference set. The primary reports underlying Chen 2023 (DR022 ref 36) and the remaining Lo Giudice trials require a separate DOI/registry check. A dependency hold does not establish formal retraction of any synthesis. [Lo Giudice, Results and reference 10](https://doi.org/10.5534/wjmh.230106)

## Pediatric and maternal overlaps
- LEAP, LEAP follow-up and the 2016 correction share one trial lineage. The original trial's negative-skin-test absolute difference is **11.8 percentage points**. A downstream NIAID guideline rationale prints 12.6 with arm percentages 13.7 and 1.9. Use the trial figure; do not reproduce the conflicting derived number. The NEJM erratum separately corrects the Figure 3 antibody ratio conversion and axis; it does not alter the Figure 2 clinical endpoint. [Trial](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1414850) · [Guideline rationale](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5226648/) · [Correction](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMx150044)
- WHO motor reference table is a data companion to the MGRS publication. A separate same-cohort growth paper (PMID 16817683) was rejected as independent motor-window evidence. WHO immediate KMC and Uganda OMWaNA are separate randomized populations. Registration NCT02811432 belongs to OMWaNA.
- Do not count the 102 parenting trials, 12 donor-milk trials, and their reviews as independent entries. Trial-level cross-review membership is pending. Use HAPO Hong Kong only as a follow-up of that cohort. PED-CORRECTION and PED-FERTILITY-HOLD supply zero independent clinical efficacy estimates.
- Reuse S086 for maternal reference tables; S215 already contains adult lactation iodine and choline. Pediatric and age-specific lactation rows in this packet are reference values, not extra independent evidence. IA006/IA007 share a choline cohort; IA008 addresses paternal supplementation in infertility. S082/S083 require omega-3 baseline status and trial overlap checks. S092/S093 require calcium dose/population/version checks. S094/S095 retain the maternal preeclampsia signal and differing trial eligibility. Neonatal live probiotic safety has a distinct source, PED-PROBIO. Preserve GE-H01–08 and RP001/RP005. Vitamin D evidence must separate deficiency treatment, total intake reference and supplementation trials. Audit dose-regimen versions and exclusions before promoting benefits.
- Excluded discovery examples: the FAZST design publication (PMID 31712803) from efficacy estimates; the 1999 folate guidance as a current recommendation; a nongovernmental mirror of the fetal encyclopedia; an unrelated blood-study supplement returned by the LEAP query. No total rejected-screening count is available.
