## Hospital skin-to-skin

Ask the neonatal team how to take part in skin-to-skin care.

The multicountry trial found lower 28-day mortality. The Ugandan trial had a less precise 28-day estimate and found similar 7-day mortality across groups. Care environments and eligible infants differed. Staff determine positioning, monitoring and readiness. Routine home sleep guidance still applies.

**Ask the care team:** How will staff support positioning and monitor our baby?

[Immediate "Kangaroo Mother Care" and Survival of Infants with Low Birth Weight](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34038632/) · [Effectiveness of kangaroo mother care before clinical stabilisation versus standard care among neonates at five hospitals in Uganda (OMWaNA): a parallel-group, individually randomised controlled trial and economic evaluation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38754454/) · [Sleep-Related Infant Deaths: Updated 2022 Recommendations for Reducing Infant Deaths in the Sleep Environment.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35726558/)

## Newborn screening

Ask who will communicate the blood spot, hearing and heart screening results.

Each screen has its own follow-up. An out-of-range result needs the program's next test. A passing oxygen screen misses some heart conditions. Breathing difficulty or an unresponsive baby requires immediate attention even after a reassuring screen.

**Ask the care team:** Who owns follow-up if we leave before results come back?

[Newborn Screening Results and Follow-Up | Newborn Screening](https://newbornscreening.hrsa.gov/newborn-screening-process/newborn-screening-results-and-follow) · [When to Call Emergency Medical Services (EMS) for Your Child - HealthyChildren.org](https://www.healthychildren.org/english/health-issues/injuries-emergencies/pages/when-to-call-emergency-medical-services-ems.aspx)

## Vitamin K

Ask the birth team about the vitamin K injection to prevent dangerous bleeding.

Babies have low vitamin K stores. Bleeding can begin without visible warning. Birth-team intervention; do not substitute dietary intake or create a home dosing plan.

**Ask the care team:** How is the injection recorded in the discharge summary?

[About Vitamin K Deficiency Bleeding | Vitamin K Deficiency Bleeding | CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/vitamin-k-deficiency/about/)

## Jaundice

A jaundiced newborn who feeds poorly or is hard to wake needs emergency department assessment now.

Feeding and alertness can change the urgency of jaundice. A visual comparison cannot estimate bilirubin safely. If the infant cannot be awakened, call US 911 immediately.

**Ask the care team:** When is the next bilirubin or feeding check?

[symptomviewer - HealthyChildren.org](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/tips-tools/symptom-checker/Pages/symptomviewer.aspx?symptom=Jaundiced+Newborn) · [When to Call Emergency Medical Services (EMS) for Your Child - HealthyChildren.org](https://www.healthychildren.org/english/health-issues/injuries-emergencies/pages/when-to-call-emergency-medical-services-ems.aspx)

## Fever

Call the pediatrician immediately for a rectal temperature of 38°C (100.4°F) or higher in a baby 3 months or younger.

An infant can look well despite serious infection. Chronological age governs this escalation. Unresponsive or blue/gray lips: call US 911. Avoid waiting for fever medicine or substituting corrected age.

**Ask the care team:** Who handles urgent calls out of hours?

[Fever and Your Baby - HealthyChildren.org](https://www.healthychildren.org/english/health-issues/conditions/fever/pages/fever-and-your-baby.aspx) · [When to Call Emergency Medical Services (EMS) for Your Child - HealthyChildren.org](https://www.healthychildren.org/english/health-issues/injuries-emergencies/pages/when-to-call-emergency-medical-services-ems.aspx)

## Sleep environment

Place babies on their backs for sleep on a firm, flat, separate sleep surface without loose bedding.

Room sharing allows closeness while maintaining a separate infant sleep space. Inclines and soft surfaces create hazards. Use the infant's discharge plan for unusual medical needs. A car seat or a caregiver's chest is not a routine sleep surface.

**Ask the care team:** Where can we set up a safe surface for naps and nighttime feeds?

[Sleep-Related Infant Deaths: Updated 2022 Recommendations for Reducing Infant Deaths in the Sleep Environment.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35726558/)

## Breastfeeding support

Families who want to breastfeed can ask for hands-on feeding support. Formula and combination feeding also deserve practical support.

The Belarus trial randomized facilities to support; GI illness was less common in intervention facilities. The respiratory infection estimate was inconclusive. The study cannot assign an individual child's risk from feeding choice. Seek help with poor intake or growth.

**Ask the care team:** What feeding plan will support both intake and caregiver wellbeing?

[Promotion of Breastfeeding Intervention Trial (PROBIT): a randomized trial in the Republic of Belarus](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11242425/) · [Infant Formula Preparation and Storage | Infant and Toddler Nutrition | CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/infant-toddler-nutrition/formula-feeding/preparation-and-storage.html) · [Storage and Preparation of Breast Milk](https://www.cdc.gov/breastfeeding/pdf/preparation-of-breast-milk_h.pdf)

## Formula preparation

Use safe water and the exact proportions on the formula label. Discard formula left after feeding.

For prepared formula use the earlier applicable limit: two hours after preparation at room temperature or one hour after feeding begins. Refrigerate promptly if feeding has not begun and use within 24 hours. Powder is not sterile. Under 2 months, premature birth or weakened immunity requires extra precautions; use the CDC high-risk instructions or treating team.

**Ask the care team:** Do our water supply, product type or medical history change preparation?

[Infant Formula Preparation and Storage | Infant and Toddler Nutrition | CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/infant-toddler-nutrition/formula-feeding/preparation-and-storage.html)

## Expressed milk

Label milk with the expression time and use the oldest suitable milk first.

Fresh milk can be stored up to four hours at 77°F/25°C or colder or four days at 40°F/4°C or colder. Use refrigerated thawed milk within 24 hours of complete thawing. Hospital and NICU instructions can differ. Never refreeze fully thawed milk.

**Ask the care team:** Which storage guidance applies after discharge?

[Storage and Preparation of Breast Milk](https://www.cdc.gov/breastfeeding/pdf/preparation-of-breast-milk_h.pdf)

## Probiotic safety

Ask the neonatal team before considering any live probiotic for a premature infant.

FDA documented an invasive infection and death involving a product organism. The alert supplies no population risk denominator. Do not generalize findings across strains, gestational ages or indications. No consumer product recommendation.

**Ask the care team:** What are the strain-specific evidence and infection controls?

[Risk of Invasive Disease in Preterm Infants Given Probiotics Formulated to Contain Live Bacteria or Yeast | FDA](https://www.fda.gov/safety/medical-product-safety-information/risk-invasive-disease-preterm-infants-given-probiotics-formulated-contain-live-bacteria-or-yeast)

## Hearing follow-up

Ask for the hearing screen result and the date of any follow-up appointment.

US program goals are screening before one month, diagnostic evaluation before three months, and early intervention enrollment before six months when indicated. A nonpassing screen needs follow-up and is not a diagnosis. Later concerns warrant evaluation after a pass.

**Ask the care team:** Who schedules diagnostic audiology?

## Donor milk in neonatal care

When a very preterm infant needs additional milk, the neonatal team can discuss screened donor milk.

Across 11 trials, donor milk lowered NEC risk. Infection and mortality estimates included little or no difference. Applies to neonatal units. Excludes informal milk sharing and healthy term infant feeding comparisons.

**Ask the care team:** Which feeding options and fortification plan fit this baby's growth and clinical needs?

[Donor human milk for preventing necrotising enterocolitis in very preterm or very low-birthweight infants](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD002971_does-donor-human-milk-prevent-severe-bowel-disorders-very-preterm-or-very-low-birthweight-infants)
