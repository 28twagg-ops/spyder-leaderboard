# infant and early childhood

Research draft. See the structured claim for stage, certainty and safety exceptions.

## Settling choices

Families can discuss bedtime fading or graduated settling when sleep is difficult.

A small trial found shorter sleep onset and did not detect attachment differences at follow-up. Statistical power for uncommon harms was limited. Do not extrapolate to newborns, prescribe stopping night feeds or change safe sleep arrangements.

**Ask the care team:** Could feeding or a health issue be contributing to waking?

[Behavioral Interventions for Infant Sleep Problems: A Randomized Controlled Trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27221288/) · [Sleep-Related Infant Deaths: Updated 2022 Recommendations for Reducing Infant Deaths in the Sleep Environment.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35726558/)

## Starting foods

Introduce developmentally suitable foods alongside milk around six months, including foods that supply iron.

Follow readiness and feeding cues. Iron-fortified formula, meat and iron-rich complementary foods contribute differently to total intake. No cereal in a bottle for sleep. Preterm birth, feeding difficulty, or restricted diets merit an individual assessment.

**Ask the care team:** Which textures and iron sources fit our child now?

[WHO Guideline for complementary feeding of infants and young children 6-23 months of age](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240081864) · [Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) | Infant and Toddler Nutrition | CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/infant-toddler-nutrition/faqs/index.html) · [Iron | Infant and Toddler Nutrition | CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/infant-toddler-nutrition/vitamins-minerals/iron.html)

## Peanut prevention

Discuss peanut introduction early if a baby has severe eczema or egg allergy.

Regular consumption in selected infants decreased allergy at age five. Later follow-up assessed the same children. Prevention and management of established allergy are separate topics. No whole nuts, home challenge, automatic eligibility or study-dose conversion. Prior reaction needs clinical assessment.

**Ask the care team:** Should introduction be supervised for this child?

[Randomized trial of peanut consumption in infants at risk for peanut allergy.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25705822/) · [Follow-up to Adolescence after Early Peanut Introduction for Allergy Prevention](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38804779/) · [Addendum Guidelines for the](https://www.niaid.nih.gov/sites/default/files/addendum-peanut-allergy-prevention-guidelines.pdf) · [Randomized Trial of Peanut Consumption in Infants at Risk for Peanut Allergy](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMx150044)

## Responding and playing

Use everyday moments to notice what interests your child and respond. Support programs can help families practice.

Trials of parenting programs showed average benefits across developmental measures, with considerable variation. A pooled score does not predict a child's abilities. Adapt communication and play to disability, language and fatigue. Particular toys or programs have no demonstrated advantage in this dataset.

**Ask the care team:** What low-cost activities fit our child's communication style?

[Parenting interventions to promote early child development in the first three years of life: A global systematic review and meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33970913/)

## Motor variation

Look for progress over time and discuss concerns even when a skill falls within a broad reference window.

In the reference study, 35 of 816 children did not show hands-and-knees crawling. The published first and 99th percentiles describe variation. Use corrected age when interpreting early preterm development. Regression or concern warrants assessment regardless of percentile.

**Ask the care team:** Would developmental screening or early intervention be appropriate?

[WHO Motor Development Study: windows of achievement for six gross motor development milestones](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16817682/) · [# Windows of achievement for six gross motor milestones](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/child-growth/child-growth-standards/indicators/motor-development-milestones/mm_windows_table.pdf) · [Your Preemie's Growth & Developmental Milestones - HealthyChildren.org](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/preemie/pages/Preemie-Milestones.aspx) · [Clinical Screening for Autism Spectrum Disorder | Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) | CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/autism/hcp/diagnosis/screening.html)

## Growth and screening

Review growth trends and developmental concerns at well visits.

US guidance uses WHO charts before age two. Developmental screening is recommended at 9, 18 and 30 months, and autism screening at 18 and 24 months. An app checklist cannot diagnose delay. A concern can prompt assessment between visits.

**Ask the care team:** Which chart and screening tool are appropriate for our child?

[Summary: Using WHO Child Growth Standards | Growth Chart Training | CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/growth-chart-training/hcp/using-growth-charts/who-summary.html) · [Clinical Screening for Autism Spectrum Disorder | Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) | CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/autism/hcp/diagnosis/screening.html) · [Your Preemie's Growth & Developmental Milestones - HealthyChildren.org](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/preemie/pages/Preemie-Milestones.aspx)

## RSV and immunization

Ask whether maternal RSV vaccination or an infant preventive antibody applies this season.

Maternal and infant protection pathways overlap. Most infants do not need both. The infant antibody is passive protection. Eligibility depends on timing, season and medical history. Verify current jurisdictional guidance before release.

**Ask the care team:** Which prevention has already been given and where is it documented?

[RSV Immunization Guidance for Infants and Young Children | RSV | CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/rsv/hcp/vaccine-clinical-guidance/infants-young-children.html) · [Vaccines and Other Immunizing Agents in the Child and Adolescent Immunization Schedule* | Vaccines & Immunizations | CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-schedules/child-schedule-vaccines.html)

## Lead in older housing

Ask about peeling paint and renovation dust where children spend time.

Older US housing can contain lead paint. Exposure assessment can identify hazards before expensive product replacements. Seek professional assessment for suspected exposure and avoid disturbing suspect paint.

**Ask the care team:** Should the child have a blood lead test?

[About Lead in Paint | Childhood Lead Poisoning Prevention | CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/lead-prevention/prevention/paint.html)

## Travel and water

Keep a child rear-facing up to the seat manufacturer's rear-facing height or weight limit. Use close supervision around water.

Seat fit depends on the particular child and model. Four-sided isolation fencing adds protection against unintended pool access. Swimming ability and barriers do not replace adult supervision. Refer to the actual seat and vehicle manuals.

**Ask the care team:** Where can we have the seat installation checked?

[How to Install Rear-Facing Car Seats | NHTSA](https://www.nhtsa.gov/car-seats-and-booster-seats/how-install-rear-facing-car-seats) · [Drowning Prevention for Curious Toddlers: What Parents Need to Know - HealthyChildren.org](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/safety-prevention/at-play/Pages/Water-Safety-And-Young-Children.aspx)

## First teeth

Begin brushing when the first tooth appears.

AAP describes a rice-grain smear of fluoride toothpaste before age three. Adult help matters while children are learning. Water fluoride content and supplemental fluoride are separate clinical questions.

**Ask the care team:** When should we establish a dental home?

[Good Oral Health Starts Early: AAP Policy Explained - HealthyChildren.org](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/healthy-living/oral-health/Pages/Brushing-Up-on-Oral-Health-Never-Too-Early-to-Start.aspx)

## Caregiver overwhelm

When crying feels overwhelming, place the baby in a safe crib and take a brief break. Ask another caregiver for help.

Planning a safe handoff protects the baby and gives caregivers time to recover. Never shake a baby. Suspected injury needs urgent professional assessment; unresponsive baby needs EMS.

**Ask the care team:** Who can give us relief when crying becomes difficult?

[About Abusive Head Trauma | Child Abuse and Neglect Prevention | CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/child-abuse-neglect/about/about-abusive-head-trauma.html) · [Sleep-Related Infant Deaths: Updated 2022 Recommendations for Reducing Infant Deaths in the Sleep Environment.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35726558/)

## Prenatal and infant RSV protection

The maternal vaccine trial found fewer severe infant illnesses. An infant antibody trial found fewer hospitalizations during the first RSV season. The two trials enrolled different populations and used different endpoints and controls. Their efficacy percentages do not compare the products head-to-head.

FDA identifies uncertainty about a preterm-birth imbalance. Current gestational window, season and maternal vaccination history matter. No automatic product choice.

**Ask the care team:** Which option fits the timing of our pregnancy or birth?

[Bivalent Prefusion F Vaccine in Pregnancy to Prevent RSV Illness in Infants](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37018474/) · [Nirsevimab for Prevention of Hospitalizations Due to RSV in Infants](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38157500/) · [ABRYSVO prescribing information](https://www.fda.gov/media/168889/download) · [RSV Immunization Guidance for Infants and Young Children | RSV | CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/rsv/hcp/vaccine-clinical-guidance/infants-young-children.html)

## Preventing eczema with moisturizer

Daily moisturizer has produced different prevention results in two large trials. Families need separate advice for treating existing eczema. US CASCADE found fewer diagnoses over two years; UK BEEP found no clear reduction in eczema present at age two in high-risk infants. Population, duration and outcome definitions differed.

BEEP reported increased skin infection rates. The evidence does not support universal product claims or stopping prescribed treatment.

**Ask the care team:** Is the aim to treat existing dry or inflamed skin, or to prevent a future diagnosis?

[Daily emollient during infancy for prevention of eczema: the BEEP randomised controlled trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32087126/) · [Emollients to Prevent Pediatric Eczema: A Randomized Clinical Trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40699587/)

## Starting solids with a spoon or self-feeding

Families can use responsive spoon-feeding or developmentally appropriate self-feeding and ask for help with safe textures. A supported baby-led program did not clearly improve BMI z scores. Its choking report came from the same families. It could not exclude differences in rare severe events.

Gagging and choking require different interpretation. The trial included safety coaching; results cannot establish that a food shape is safe or that an infant with swallowing difficulty is eligible.

**Ask the care team:** Which textures can our child manage safely?

[Effect of a Baby-Led Approach to Complementary Feeding on Infant Growth and Overweight: A Randomized Clinical Trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28692728/) · [A Baby-Led Approach to Eating Solids and Risk of Choking](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27647715/) · [WHO Guideline for complementary feeding of infants and young children 6-23 months of age](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240081864) · [Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) | Infant and Toddler Nutrition | CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/infant-toddler-nutrition/faqs/index.html)
