# Research methods and release gates

This staged extension uses public research only. PED identifiers are local to this packet. Join sources.json to studies.jsonl by source_id and to claims-and-content.jsonl by source_ids. A study row describes a report, cohort groups identify nonindependent follow-ups, and review rows do not add participants to trial-level counts. All evidence and safety judgments are preliminary. Guidance includes clinical and public education resources; it has no invented sample size or trial effect. A blank in CSV or null in JSON represents an unknown or inapplicable field. RIS is a discovery export; use sources.json for authoritative links and publication metadata.

Use a prespecified PICO for every future comparison and a separate harms question. Compare patient-important outcomes, maturation measures and laboratory markers separately. Record baseline risk, clinical setting, registration, affected endpoints in notices, overlap, follow-up completeness and individual disclosures before promoting a record. For numeric rows, use reported adjusted estimates where available. Never reconstruct denominators from rounded proportions. Percentile bounds describe the distribution. Confidence limits on those bounds describe estimation uncertainty.

The nutrient CSV keeps the official age bands, including 4–8 years for a 4- or 5-year-old. It records selected AI/RDA columns only. Health Canada publishes joint US/Canadian reference values; preserve table footnotes and cross-check current US NIH references before release. Total water includes milk and food moisture; never use this row as a plain-water target for an infant. Protein values use g/day, not per-kg dosing. Do not infer supplemental need, toxicity, or an absence of a limit from this table. Review formulation-specific ULs (for example preformed vitamin A, supplemental magnesium and folate forms), maternal age, premature birth and clinical exceptions separately. Existing pregnancy rows remain authoritative within the parent project.

Release gates: (1) pediatric clinician verifies each claim and escalation with citation, subgroup, source version and jurisdiction; (2) second extractor checks source tables, direction, unit, denominator, registration and study disclosures; (3) resolve relevant retraction dependencies and version conflicts; (4) clinical and accessibility review of captions, quick warnings and media rights; (5) execute safety cases through actual iPhone Home Screen, browser, offline and cross-device entry points; (6) verify local privacy and no personal data in exports; (7) explicitly authorize promotion into the canonical manifest. An offline vaccine schedule requires a dated version and a way to identify stale eligibility content. No release gate has been clinically signed off.
