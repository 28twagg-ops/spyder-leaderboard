# Pediatric safety rules · PED-001
All records are US educational research drafts. Every family-facing release requires pediatric review, including the exact visible wording, source revision, age limits and offline behavior. Emergency copy requires qualified emergency/pediatric review. Each scenario is synthetic. Never collect or commit a child's dates, measurements, photos, diagnoses or feeding log for testing.

Use gestational weeks for pregnancy, chronological age since birth for acute illness, and corrected age for interpreting premature developmental progress where appropriate (PED-CORRECTED). Keep birthweight eligibility, current weight, gestational age, chronological age and developmental readiness as different fields. Unknown values cannot select a risk pathway. Corrected age cannot relax a fever threshold. Explain variability and honor caregiver concerns even inside a reference range.

Show the highest applicable urgency immediately. US emergency symptoms go directly to 911. Preserve written action plans for allergy and instructions from the treating NICU. Never delay an emergency while collecting measurements, reading a chart, answering a quiz or opening an external resource. A reassuring screen cannot override symptoms. Do not diagnose, clear feeding challenges, offer medication calculations, compute supplement amounts from total intake references, or produce individualized vaccine schedules. High-risk records remain blocked from publishing, notifications and action automation. No risk classifications based on funding alone.

Any preterm, immunocompromised or medically complex child, swallowing difficulty, significant allergy, growth concern or specialty formula needs individualized clinician input. Avoid comparison scores, countdowns to milestones, purchasable developmental promises and coercive feeding copy. Unknown harms or missing disclosures stay unknown. No external link may receive a child identifier.
