# What matters when

The early bands are organizational choices. General dental care, fitness and chronic-condition planning can begin years ahead. Product trials during pregnancy do not demonstrate any advantage to starting years earlier. The clearest time-sensitive example is periconceptional folic acid. Gestational weeks and postpartum months use different clocks.

**Urgent at any stage of pregnancy or during the year after birth.** Seek immediate medical care for warning signs including severe or worsening headache, vision changes, fever of 100.4°F/38°C or higher, trouble breathing, chest pain, heavy bleeding, or thoughts of harming yourself or the baby. For life-threatening symptoms call local emergency services (911 in the US). Tell the team about the pregnancy or recent delivery. This short list is illustrative. [Full CDC warning signs](https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/index.html)

**Pathways.** After assisted vaginal delivery or severe tearing, prioritize perineal healing, continence and pain. After cesarean, prioritize incision assessment, comfortable movement and clot warning signs. After any complicated pregnancy, use the discharge team’s condition-specific follow-up. During lactation, consider infant exposure, milk supply and diet in the relevant record. Formula and combination feeding remain valid choices. For twins and later pregnancies, verify the study population before transferring estimates.
