## Composition contracts
Home: small brand with botanical mark, Saved/Settings, restrained serif journey heading with integrated botanical, prominent current phase and change control, three compact illustrated reading rows based on actual selected phase (not recommendations), secondary shortcuts, visible urgent access and draft disclaimer. Use honest neutral defaults if no selection. No default giant title, giant grid or long inventories. 
Onboarding: concise native-style sheet, compact clear choices, visible skip, two-step phase selection, dismissal and persistent optional reading filter. Avoid default focus landing on an oversized close box; keep keyboard focus accessible. Fit normal and short iPhone viewport with internal scroll only if needed, skip reachable; honor user-selected theme and local privacy.
Journey: visual sequence, varied stage imagery, timeline rail and individual stage/week detail; no full catalog by default. Explore: meaningful interactive body map and accessible equivalent controls; categories compact; anatomical asset only with exact rights and review, never imply diagnosis. Reader: restrained editorial title, quick reading navigation, save control, visible safety and draft context, research details expandable. No display of blocked conclusions as guidance or fabricated charts. Search: familiar field, few suggestions and clear results; evidence/visuals: compact collections, source details on demand, no automatically loaded unapproved imagery. Settings: simple grouped preferences, clear local data controls. Urgent page: direct, accessible from every main screen, no delay or AI triage.
