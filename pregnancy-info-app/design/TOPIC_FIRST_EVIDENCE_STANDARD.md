Latest owner direction, 2026-11-27. The weekly screen is a compact, visual topic directory, not a long article. Separate Mother and Baby. Aim for no vertical scroll at standard phone sizes, with accessible scrolling allowed at small viewports or enlarged text. Each topic opens a focused subsection directory, with simple explanations, diagrams, interventions, evidence and safety in context. No generic "additional reading" or standalone research menu is required to understand the selected subject. Do not remove existing archive or evidence-browser access.

## Bias and funding integrated into every claim
Use [Compare Evidence Standard](../research/staged-completion/COMPARE_EVIDENCE_STANDARD.md), [Funding Standard](../research/staged-completion/stage-01/FUNDING_STANDARD.md), and [Independent Evidence Review](../research/INDEPENDENT_EVIDENCE_REVIEW.md). These rules apply to all domains, conventional and alternative treatments alike. No fixed reading-grade requirement; clarity first.

- Each material claim gets numbered footnotes pointing to the exact source and locator. Show relevant funding and uncertainty beside the conclusion, then expand to study details.
- Each study card preserves population, intervention, actual comparator (placebo, no treatment, standard care or another active intervention), sample sizes/attrition, outcome, follow-up, estimates/intervals and harms. Use comparable data only, no invented denominator or chart. Existing chart holds remain in force.
- Separate direct commercial funding, product donations/in-kind support, author financial interests, sponsor roles, verified noncommercial support, unclear disclosures and not-applicable categories. Funding unknown is never independent. Show reported ties and missing evidence rather than speculate about misconduct. Evaluate methodology separately (randomization, blinding, attrition, prespecified outcomes, selective reporting, replication, population relevance and uncertainty). No arbitrary bias score.
- Default synthesis prioritizes direct, well-conducted and independently replicated findings when available; preserve all eligible evidence including negative sponsor-related findings and harms. Offer All evidence, verified no identified relevant commercial ties, commercial direct, mixed/in-kind, unclear, and separate quality filters where data permit. No "completely unbiased" label. No automatic exclusion based solely on funding. No independent-only result when verification or comparable data are inadequate.
- Compare funding subgroups only within a defined deduplicated eligible set and matched outcome; include exact denominator, source IDs, method, uncertainty and limitations. Different statistical significance does not establish subgroup interaction. Reviews and underlying trials are not independent counts; alias groups and overlapping cohorts need reconciliation. Never apply a reported adult cosmetic outcome to pregnancy prevention.
- Show safety and urgency before optional detail. Hold RP001 (unsafe pregnancy recommendation) and RP005 (text/table discrepancy), all other existing gates and qualified-review requirements. Never turn a research comparison into an automated treatment or dosing plan.

## Example — research presentation candidate only
Osman et al. 2008 (S004/P3-02, same trial), PMID 18715434: reported new stretch marks 45.1% active cocoa butter lotion vs 48.8% placebo lotion, p=0.730; 175/210 completed. The result does not establish a prevention benefit. Cochrane trial description records E.T. Browne Drug Company donation of both lotions and university/Wellcome support; exact sponsor control remains unverified. Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18715434/ and https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10001689/ . Do not create a clinical chart until reconciliation and review gate passed.
Myung & Park 2025 (S011/P3-05, same review), PMID 40324552: overall adult skin results differed by pharmaceutical-company funding and quality subgroups. Subgroups without pharmaceutical-company funding and higher-quality studies did not show significant benefits; this does not prove causal funding bias or pregnancy prevention. Authors declare no conflict, review funding needs verification. Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40324552/ . Preserve original review and overlapping reanalyses.

## Visual-first evidence presentation — latest owner feedback
Prefer meaningful figures, labeled diagrams, comparable outcome charts and compact funding/quality comparisons over lengthy prose. Show one plain-language takeaway, then optional methods, funding details, limitations and numbered citations. Use actual study values and scales only; qualitative findings use labeled comparisons rather than invented numerical bars. Do not imply that statistical significance proves clinical benefit or funding causation. Keep essential safety visible and retain all chart publication/review gates. This is a design requirement, not an approval to deploy unreviewed charts.
