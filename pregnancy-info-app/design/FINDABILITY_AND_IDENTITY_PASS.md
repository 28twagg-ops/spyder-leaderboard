## Scope
Home is unblocked on launch. Direct search and popular topics. Timeline named plainly and all forty weeks exposed with visible number grid. Explore presents all subjects without extra collection layers; legacy routes preserved. Focused reading search adds all 98 focused drafts, mapped topic/skin readings and weekly feature aliases; deduplicates routes, leaves held source gates unchanged. Shared topics use user's selected calendar week where present; stage-specific reference results show contextual week. Direct detailed results use actual Back when entered from Search, Home or Explore; retain breadcrumbs and week context.

Optional EDD or current completed weeks + days, calendar-only 280-day arithmetic, no clinical dating, prediction or automatic birth state. Save locally, allow edits/removal, display privacy and storage failures, clear on explicitly switching life stages. No cloud sync, network transmission, health scoring or tracking. Dates may be lost when a web app is deleted. No date is required.

Identity: original two nested cradle curves with warm seed, full-bleed olive, ivory and clay; mark is not trademark-cleared. Matching versioned SVG/PNG favicon, Apple touch 180px and 192/512 PWA icon, maskable padding. May require re-adding app; do not promise existing iOS icon refresh. Preserve old paths for compatibility.
