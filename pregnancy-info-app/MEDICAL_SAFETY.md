# Medical safety — nonnegotiable

Educational research, not individualized diagnosis, treatment or clearance. Maternal and fetal health, informed choice, comfort and function take priority over cosmetic preservation. No guarantee of avoiding normal physical changes. Respect the pregnant person's decisions and ability to opt out.

Assess timing separately: years before conception, immediately before, trying, each trimester, delivery, immediate/later postpartum, breastfeeding and after breastfeeding. Unknown pregnancy safety is unknown, not safe. State washout or restrictions only with credible supporting sources. Safety warnings remain visible in quick view. Higher-risk interventions, medications, supplements, invasive or experimental procedures and individualized advice require appropriate clinician involvement. Do not provide home invasive protocols, experimental doses, unsafe exercise instructions, restrictive dieting or inadequate weight gain advice. Do not steer delivery or feeding choices based on unsupported cosmetic claims.

Postpartum progression must be based on individual healing, symptoms, delivery and professional guidance rather than fixed dates. Pelvic floor care may require relaxation as well as strengthening. Clearly distinguish prevention, treatment, cosmetic concerns and medically urgent problems.

Include source-backed urgent maternal warning signs and appropriate emergency guidance in relevant sections; verify current guidance before publication. Avoid alarmist presentation and do not suggest delaying care. Do not use appearance scoring, compulsive tracking or body-shaming language. Keep personal plans optional and editable.

No AI-generated clinical claims become verified simply because structural tests pass. Use substantive source checks and suitable professional review; high-risk content remains research-only until review. Document uncertainties, limitations and conflicts.
