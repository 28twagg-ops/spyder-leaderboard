## 4. Research execution
Use research/OPEN_SOURCE_DISCOVERY_STANDARD.md in addition to the existing independent appraisal directive. Prioritize neutral questions and original data, including credible evidence contrary to guidelines and alternative claims, clinical study reports, protocols, preprints, dissertations, trial registries, safety data, qualitative accounts, local and international research, relevant podcasts/books/community reports as leads. No automatic preference based on institutional prestige, marketing, accreditation, popularity or lack thereof. Funding and methodological bias are separate. Record quality of methods and uncertainty rather than claim absolute non-bias. Do not recommend an unverified intervention just because it is natural, independent or outside mainstream medicine; do not dismiss it for those reasons either. Missing evidence is an explicit unknown. Clinical safety controls are not optional.
