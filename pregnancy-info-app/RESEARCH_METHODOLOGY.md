# Research methodology — seed standard

Use structured literature discovery and appraisal. Source targets: PubMed, Europe PMC, Cochrane, Crossref, ClinicalTrials.gov, ACOG, AAD, APTA, WHO, NICE, RCOG, MotherToBaby, LactMed, NIH ODS, independent universities, international guidelines and appropriate specialist literature. Books, podcasts, videos, patient accounts, traditional approaches and communities may generate hypotheses or explain lived experience, not establish clinical effectiveness. Respect access and licenses; do not copy full copyrighted materials.

For every question: define population/intervention/comparator/outcome and stage; document search date, databases, queries, inclusion/exclusion and screening decisions; collect and deduplicate underlying studies (not just publications); extract data, methods, funding and conflicts; compare relevant guidelines; assess risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision and reporting/publication bias; assess safety separately; synthesize plain-language and detailed views from common records; verify citations against actual source passages; record review/update dates and change history.

Include non-pharmaceutical and unconventional research on the same merits as commercial research. Do not treat independence as proof or commercial funding as automatic disqualification. Compare funding subgroups when data support it. Do not conflate mechanistic plausibility or anecdotes with human outcomes. Null results with wide intervals are not proof of no benefit. If evidence or pregnancy safety is missing, mark unknown. If credible evidence demonstrates harm or ineffectiveness, retain a clearly labeled explanatory record, not a recommended action.

Record title, authors, publication year, journal, DOI/PMID/URL, study design, sample size, population, comparison, intervention, duration, endpoints, effect size, confidence interval, limitations, funding, conflicts, replication, pregnancy applicability, full-text/abstract status, correction/retraction and last verification. Cite every substantive medical claim. Never invent sources, counts, quotes or results.

Research status: unresearched → searching → extracted → verification pending → reviewed → published → update required. AI completion is not clinical review. Require appropriate human review for high-risk or individualized advice; keep it research-only until reviewed. If web access is missing, log blocker rather than manufacture findings. Claude establishes exact appraisal and source verification methods before delegation.
