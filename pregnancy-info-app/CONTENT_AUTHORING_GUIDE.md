# Content authoring guide

1. Prefer source IDs from stage-10 consolidated import — do not invent studies.
2. Write layer 1 at ~grade 7–8; keep denser detail in expandable layers.
3. Never paste archived AI conclusions into family-facing copy.
4. Record independent appraisal before comparing prior AI text.
5. Preserve all holds; unknown funding stays unknown.
6. Mark synthetic fixtures with `publicationStatus: synthetic_demo`.
7. Urgent signs stay visible; no buried emergencies.
8. Feeding, birth, appearance, and developmental outcomes must not become success scores.
