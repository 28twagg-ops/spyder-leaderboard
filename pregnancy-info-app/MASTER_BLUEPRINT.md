# Master blueprint — requirements v0.1

## Purpose
Create an extensive evidence-led personal preparation, pregnancy and recovery resource with a configurable approximately three-year preconception horizon. Goals: preserve health, comfort, function and appearance where reasonably possible; understand normal/genetic/hormonal changes; prevent or manage preventable complications; enable informed treatment decisions and recovery. No promise of unchanged appearance. Maternal and fetal health and the pregnant person's autonomy override cosmetic priorities.

## Two reading experiences
Default quick guide: visual takeaways, very little text, a handful of high-value priorities, effort/time estimates, visible safety caveats. Optional expandable practical detail, then scientific analysis and original sources. Advanced research explorer: study comparisons, methods, outcomes, uncertainty, funding, source inspection and filters. Both views use the same verified structured data.

## Relative chronology
36–24, 24–18, 18–12, 12–6, 6–3, 3–1 months preconception; final month; actively trying; trimester 1 (weeks 1–13), trimester 2 (14–27), trimester 3 (28–delivery), late pregnancy and delivery; postpartum 0–72 hours, days 4–14, weeks 2–6 and 6–12, months 3–6, 6–9, 9–12, 12–18, 18–24, beyond 24 months. These are organizational bands, not medical deadlines. Support changing dates and paths for vaginal/assisted/cesarean delivery, breastfeeding/formula/combination feeding, complications, multiple and subsequent pregnancies. Distinguish gestational age from time since conception.

## Research scope
- Skin: elasticity, collagen/elastin, stretch marks, genetics, moisturizing, cocoa/shea butter, oils, vitamin E, hyaluronic acid, Centella, massage, collagen peptides, skin nutrition, retinoids and later dermatologic treatments.
- Abdomen: linea alba, diastasis recti, strength, breathing/pressure, Pilates, binders, rehabilitation, hernia and excess skin; distinguish separation, fat, muscle and skin.
- Breast appearance: pregnancy versus feeding effects, tissue, genetics, support garments, weight variation, exercise, nonsurgical and surgical options; no cosmetic pressure about feeding.
- Fitness/body composition: resistance and aerobic exercise, mobility, healthy gestational gain, muscle retention, training modifications, symptom-based postpartum return to lifting/running.
- Pelvic floor/sexual health: coordination, strength vs relaxation, incontinence, prolapse, pain, perineal massage, biofeedback, rehabilitation and return to sexual activity.
- Nutrition: dietary patterns, protein, folate, iron, calcium, vitamins D/C/B12, choline, iodine, omega-3, magnesium, zinc, hydration, fiber, prenatal supplements, probiotics, collagen, creatine and other emerging topics; assess dose, interactions, contamination and pregnancy-specific safety.
- Face/hair/nails: acne, melasma, pigmentation, sunscreen, azelaic acid, niacinamide, retinoids, hair cycles and postpartum shedding.
- Birth: labor positions, warm compresses, perineal trauma, pushing, delivery tradeoffs, doulas and recovery paths; do not choose delivery for aesthetics alone.
- Wounds/scars: cesarean and perineal wound healing, silicone, massage when healed, sun protection, later procedures.
- Circulation: swelling, varicose/spider veins, compression, movement, red flags, later vascular treatment.
- Musculoskeletal: posture, back/hip/pelvic girdle pain, feet, ribs, joints, balance, ergonomics.
- Reproductive/general health: fertility, medication review, vaccination, dental health, family history, thyroid and relevant clinician-guided testing.
- Recovery: sleep, rest, fatigue, stress, mental health, body image, practical partner support, paternal preconception health.
- Emerging/advanced: microneedling, lasers, radiofrequency, ultrasound, red/near-infrared light, PRP, electrical stimulation, regenerative treatments, acupuncture, traditional remedies, peptides and elective surgery. Inclusion for investigation is not endorsement; no home procedural protocols or experimental dosing.

Investigate each topic by outcome and phase; separate prevention, symptom relief and treatment. Include low-cost and noncommercial methods as well as conventional treatments. Keep direct pregnancy data separate from general adult, laboratory or animal research.

## Functional modules
Today dashboard; adjustable timeline; anatomical body map; searchable research library; intervention explorer; side-by-side comparison; evidence/controversy center; preparation plan; exercise/nutrition libraries; optional private journal; appointment questions/export; partner support; source registry and research updates. Each module must serve a real purpose rather than create empty pages.

## Implementation
Claude determines the stack after inspecting environment (TypeScript/React/Next.js or another maintainable option, structured data and suitable local-first storage). Keep medical content, presentation, user data and business logic separate. Data-driven reusable pages, accessible charts, citations, progressive disclosure, validation, tests and mobile-friendly layout. Document exact choices. Keep the legacy backend unchanged.

## Definition of initial completion
Working skeleton; responsive navigation; timeline; quick/expanded views; source-backed reference content; data model; reusable scientific visualization; source registry and validation; design system; research and implementation backlogs; privacy/security and medical review workflow; tested representative routes; actual tailored handoff prompt. No claim of complete research library at this stage.
